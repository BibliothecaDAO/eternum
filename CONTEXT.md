# Eternum Game

This glossary defines the game-specific language used when discussing armies, mounted creatures, combat presentation,
and tier progression.

## Mounted armies

**Paladin**: A mounted troop archetype whose rider and mount form one army presentation and one authoritative combatant.
_Avoid_: Horse unit, cavalry model

**Mount**: The ridden creature half of a Paladin presentation. A mount supplies the rider's saddle relationship and may
use ground or flight locomotion. _Avoid_: Horse, when referring to every possible ridden creature

**Mount Form**: The tier-specific creature identity in the Paladin progression: T1 Courser, T2 Warhorse, or T3 Sky
Dragon. _Avoid_: Horse tier, skin

**Ground Mount**: A mount form that presents movement through terrain-contact locomotion. The Courser and Warhorse are
ground mounts. _Avoid_: Walking skin

**Flying Mount**: A mount form that presents movement above the terrain while retaining the army's authoritative map
path. The Sky Dragon is a flying mount. _Avoid_: Flying unit, when no gameplay traversal rule changes

## Combat presentation

**Mount Action**: The mount-owned visual performance synchronized with an authoritative Paladin attack. _Avoid_: Mount
ability, unless it introduces distinct gameplay rules

**Fire Breath**: The Sky Dragon's mount action: a directed breath effect that presents the existing Paladin attack
without defining a separate damage or area-of-effect rule. _Avoid_: Fire spell, dragon ability
