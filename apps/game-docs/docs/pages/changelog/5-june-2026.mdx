# 5th June 2026

### Combat v3

- Crossbowmen now have a distinct ranged role: they can attack from **2 hexes away**, while Knights and Paladins remain
  adjacent-only.
- Range-2 Crossbowman attacks are ranged pokes: **70%** damage into field armies, **30%** damage into structure guards,
  no biome damage modifier, no defender counter-damage, shorter battle timers, and no normal damage-ratio refunds.
- Range-2 Crossbowman attacks cost **50 stamina** for the attacker and **20 stamina** for the defender. Range-1 combat
  remains **50 stamina** for the attacker and **40 stamina** for the defender.
- Ranged attacks can clear structure guards, but structures still require adjacency to claim.
- Knights now specialize in structure combat: **+15%** damage when assaulting structure guards from adjacent range, and
  Knight guards take **15% reduced incoming damage** while defending structures.
- Paladins are unchanged mechanically and remain high-mobility cavalry.
- T2 and T3 troop strength multipliers are standardized at **3×** and **9×**.

### Blitz Stamina & Relic Consistency

- [Blitz/Stamina & Biomes](/blitz/military/stamina-and-biomes): Clarified Blitz stamina regeneration as **30 stamina per
  phase** and **180 stamina per Eternum Day**.
- Confirmed combat stamina thresholds are config-driven values, not dynamically derived at runtime from regeneration.
- [Blitz/Relics](/blitz/materials/relics): Standardized all timed relic effects to **3 Eternum Days (18 minutes)**.
- Updated Troop Production Relics to **+15%** and **+30%** troop production for 3 Eternum Days.
- Updated docs, generated config artifacts, shared TypeScript constants, and Cairo defaults covered by this release
  pass.
