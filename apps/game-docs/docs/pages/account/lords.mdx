# LORDS

LORDS is the Realms token. Frontier pays season rewards in LORDS.

## LORDS and test LORDS

`play.realms.party` pays in LORDS. The dev site (`play.dev-realms.party`) uses a test LORDS token with no value.

## In Frontier

- Season rewards pay LORDS to your linked payout wallet when the season ends, based on your Aspect's race standing and
  the Glory you earned in the Deep.
- {FRONTIER.shop.lordsNote} See [Shop and referrals](/frontier/shop-and-referrals).
- After the season's results are posted and checked, rewards pay out to your linked wallet. If you have no payout wallet
  linked yet, link one within {PAYOUT_WALLET.claimWindowDays} days of posting or unpaid rewards return to the treasury
  ([Payout wallet](/account/payout-wallet)).
