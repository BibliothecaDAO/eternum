# LORDS

LORDS is the Realms token. Frontier pays in it.

## LORDS and test LORDS

From the Devcon season, play.realms.party pays in LORDS. The dev game (play.dev-realms.party) runs on a test LORDS token
with no value, and you can mint it for free: under a Blitz entry, **Mint 1,000 test tokens** mints them to your payout
wallet. It takes one signature, and the wallet pays the network fee.

<Shot name="account/mint" caption="A Blitz entry short of LORDS, with Mint 1,000 test tokens under it." />

## In Frontier

- **Chests** pay LORDS into your realm ([Chests and LORDS](/frontier/chests)).
- **Refill** spends your realm's LORDS on stamina ([Armies](/frontier/armies#stamina)).
- **Withdraw** sends your realm's LORDS to your payout wallet during the season.
- **The season's end** pays what your realm still holds, and the top places' prizes, to your payout wallet. Payments are
  automatic once the payout checks complete. With no wallet linked by the claim deadline, they return to the treasury
  ([Chests and LORDS](/frontier/chests#your-lords)).
