# LORDS

LORDS is the Realms token. Frontier pays season rewards in LORDS.

## LORDS and test LORDS

`play.realms.party` pays in LORDS. The dev site (`play.dev-realms.party`) uses a test LORDS token with no value.

## In Frontier

- **Season rewards** pay LORDS to your linked payout wallet when the season ends, based on your Aspect's race standing
  and the Glory you earned in the Deep.
- **Shop** lets you buy Fragments with LORDS (or swap ETH, STRK or USDC into LORDS through AVNU) to refill stamina or
  redraw Oaths.
- **Claim deadline:** Payments are automatic once the season's results are posted and checked. If no payout wallet is
  linked, link one within {PAYOUT_WALLET.claimWindowDays} days of posting; unpaid rewards after that return to the
  treasury ([Payout wallet](/account/payout-wallet)).
