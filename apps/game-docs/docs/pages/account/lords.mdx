# LORDS

LORDS is the Realms token. Frontier pays season rewards in LORDS.

## LORDS and test LORDS

`play.realms.party` pays in LORDS. The dev site (`play.dev-realms.party`) uses a test LORDS token with no value.

## In Frontier

- After the season ends and the results are posted and checked, LORDS rewards pay out to your linked payout wallet based
  on your Aspect's race standing and the Glory you earned in the Deep. If you have no payout wallet linked yet, link one
  within {PAYOUT_WALLET.claimWindowDays} days after the results are posted or unpaid rewards return to the treasury
  ([Payout wallet](/account/payout-wallet)).
- {FRONTIER.shop.lordsNote} See [Shop and referrals](/frontier/shop-and-referrals).
