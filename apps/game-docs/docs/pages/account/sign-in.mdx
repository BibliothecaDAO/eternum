# Sign in

You sign in to Realms with Google, Apple, Discord, X, or a code sent to your email. You do not need a wallet to sign in
or play. The device you play on signs your game actions.

## Connect

Sign-in opens over the current page when you tap **Play free** or **Sign in** on the You tab. Once signed in, you return
to where you started.

<Shot name="account/connect" caption="Sign in: Google, Apple, Discord, X, or your email and Send code." />

**With Google, Apple, Discord, or X.** Press **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with
Discord**, or **Continue with X** and approve Realms there. If sign-in finishes in another browser (for example, an
in-app browser on a phone), that browser shows a code you can type back into the sign-in window.

**With email.** Type your email and press **Send code**. A six-digit code arrives by email. Enter it to sign in
automatically on the sixth digit. The sign-in window shows when the code expires and shows **New code** once it has
expired. Tap your address to change it.

<Shot name="account/code" caption="The code step: the address it went to, six boxes, and when the code expires." />

## Your name and portrait

Your first sign-in creates your Realms account. Sign-in then asks for a name, suggested from your provider or your
email, and a portrait. **Save name** saves both. You can change either later under **You → Settings**.

<Shot name="account/name" caption="A new account saves its name and picks a portrait." />

## Devices

Each browser you sign in on keeps its own key, and that key signs your game actions on that device. An account can play
on 8 devices. Entering a game on a ninth asks you to remove one. On a device that is already signed in, open **You**,
then **Wallet & Devices**, remove one device under **Devices**, and try again.

Removing a device signs it out, and it cannot enter a game until it signs in again. **Sign out** under **You →
Settings** signs this device out until you sign in again.
