# Payout wallet

Your payout wallet is the Starknet wallet linked to your Realms account. Frontier needs none to play. You need one to:

- pay a Blitz entry, and get its refund back;
- carry a Blitz rating;
- receive Blitz chests and claim the season prize;
- withdraw your Frontier realm's LORDS, and receive them and any prize when the season ends;
- found your Frontier home with a Realm the wallet holds, so it claims labor each day.

A game's results, chest and prize go to the wallet its seat was played with, even if you change your payout wallet
afterwards.

## Linking a wallet

Open **Settings**, **Payout wallet**. Every **Link wallet** in the game (a Blitz lobby, your LORDS in Frontier, your
card on You) takes you there and brings you back once it is linked.

- **I have a wallet:** Ready (formerly Argent), Braavos or Controller.
- **I need one:** Ready, in this browser with its extension, or by email with no install. On a phone, Ready by email.

Linking takes two steps: the wallet signs a message, then you type a six-digit code sent to your account's email. Realms
creates no wallet and holds no key.

## Replacing or unlinking

**Replace** links another wallet the same way. **Unlink** takes the emailed code alone, so you can unlink a wallet you
have lost. Nothing pays out until a wallet is linked.

## Network fees

Paying an entry, minting test tokens, opening a chest and claiming a prize are transactions your wallet signs, and it
pays their network fee in STRK. With no STRK for the fee, the page offers **Swap on AVNU**.
