# Payout wallet

Your payout wallet is the Starknet wallet linked to your Realms account. Frontier needs none to play. You need one to:

- pay a Blitz entry, and get its refund back;
- carry a Blitz rating;
- receive Blitz chests and claim the season prize;
- withdraw your Frontier realm's LORDS, and receive them and any prize when the season ends;
- found your Frontier home with a Realm the wallet holds, so it claims labor each day.

A game's results, chest and prize go to the wallet its seat was played with, even if you change your payout wallet
afterwards.

## Your Realms wallet

Every account can have a Realms wallet, made in one tap. After you choose your name, and at each visit until you have a
payout wallet, the game offers **Create my wallet**: your device makes a passkey, opened with your face, fingerprint or
screen lock, and that passkey owns the wallet. It becomes your payout wallet at once. If you signed in more than ten
minutes ago, you also type the six-digit code sent to your email, as for any wallet change.

There is no seed phrase. The passkey syncs to your other devices through your phone's or computer's password manager
(iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager). Realms co-signs as the wallet's guardian but can never move your LORDS on
its own: every send needs your passkey.

Your LORDS show in **Settings**, **Payout wallet**, with **Send**: paste an address (an exchange's deposit address, or
your own wallet), choose the amount, **Review**, then **Confirm with your passkey**. Realms pays the network fee, for up
to three sends a day. Blitz entries, chests and prizes paid from your Realms wallet confirm with the passkey the same
way.

### If you lose your passkey

Open **Settings**, **Payout wallet**, **Lost your passkey?** on any device you can sign in on. Make a new passkey there
and type the code sent to your email. The recovery starts at once and we email you about it; after 7 days your new
passkey owns the Realms wallet, with everything in it, at the same address.

Until then, the passkey you have now can stop it: the wallet shows **Recovery under way** with the day it ends, and
**Not you? Cancel it** confirms with that passkey. So if someone else gets into your email, you have a week to cancel.

## Linking a wallet you have

Open **Settings**, **Payout wallet**. Every **Link wallet** in the game (a Blitz lobby, your LORDS in Frontier, your
card on You) takes you there and brings you back once it is linked.

**I have a wallet:** Ready (formerly Argent), Braavos or Controller. Linking takes two steps: the wallet signs a
message, then you type a six-digit code sent to your account's email. Your Realms wallet keeps what it holds, and its
Send, after you link another.

## Replacing or unlinking

**Replace** links another wallet the same way. **Unlink** takes the emailed code alone, so you can unlink a wallet you
have lost. Nothing pays out until a wallet is linked.

Your first wallet is paid at once. After you replace it, or unlink it and link another, the first Frontier payout to the
new wallet waits {PAYOUT_WALLET.changeHoldHours} hours. A second, independent signer checks every Frontier payout, and
the wait gives you time to notice a change you did not make. Payouts to a wallet you keep never wait.

## Network fees

From your Realms wallet, Realms pays the fee. From a wallet you linked, paying an entry, minting test tokens, opening a
chest and claiming a prize are transactions your wallet signs, and it pays their network fee in STRK. With no STRK for
the fee, the page offers **Swap on AVNU**.
