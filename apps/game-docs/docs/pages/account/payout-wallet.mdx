# Payout wallet

Your payout wallet is the Starknet wallet linked to your Realms account. You can play Frontier without one. Link a
wallet when you want to:

- receive your Frontier season rewards in LORDS when the season ends;
- create your Frontier home with a Realm your wallet holds, for its daily quest bonus and its Order's Aspect;
- buy Fragments in the Shop.

## Your Realms wallet

Every account can have a Realms wallet, made in one tap. After you choose your name, and at each visit until you have a
payout wallet, the game shows **Create my wallet**. Your device makes a passkey opened with your face, fingerprint, or
screen lock, and that passkey owns the wallet. It becomes your payout wallet at once. If you signed in more than ten
minutes ago, you also type the six-digit code sent to your email, as for any wallet change.

There is no seed phrase. The passkey syncs to your other devices through your phone's or computer's password manager
(iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager). Realms co-signs as the wallet's guardian but cannot move your LORDS on its
own. Every send needs your passkey.

Your LORDS show in **Settings**, **Payout wallet**, with **Send**. Paste an address (an exchange deposit address or your
own wallet), choose the amount, tap **Review**, then **Confirm with your passkey**. Each send pays its network fee in
LORDS from the wallet, shown before you confirm. You never pay more than the shown maximum, and the unused part comes
back. **Max** sends everything but that fee.

### If you lose your passkey

Open **Settings**, **Payout wallet**, **Lost your passkey?** on any device you can sign in on. Make a new passkey there
and type the code sent to your email. Recovery starts at once and sends you an email. After 7 days, your new passkey
owns the Realms wallet and everything in it at the same address.

Until then, your current passkey can stop recovery. The wallet shows **Recovery under way** with the day it ends, and
**Not you? Cancel it** confirms with that passkey. If someone gets into your email, you have a week to cancel.

## Linking a wallet you have

Open **Settings**, **Payout wallet**. Every **Link wallet** button in the game (your LORDS in Frontier, your card on
You) takes you there and brings you back once it is linked.

Tap **I have a wallet** to connect Ready (formerly Argent), Braavos, or Controller. Linking takes two steps. First your
wallet signs a message, then you type a six-digit code sent to your account's email. Your Realms wallet keeps what it
holds, and its Send button stays available, after you link another wallet.

## Replacing or unlinking

**Replace** links another wallet the same way. **Unlink** takes the emailed code alone, so you can unlink a wallet you
have lost. Nothing pays out until a wallet is linked.

Your first wallet is paid at once. After you replace it, or unlink it and link another, the first Frontier payout to the
new wallet waits {PAYOUT_WALLET.changeHoldHours} hours. A second, independent signer checks every Frontier payout, and
the wait gives you time to notice a change you did not make. Payouts to a wallet you keep do not wait.

## Network fees

From your Realms wallet, the fee is paid in LORDS inside the same transaction. From a wallet you linked, buying
Fragments is a transaction your wallet signs, and it pays its network fee in STRK. With no STRK for the fee, the page
shows **Swap on AVNU**.
