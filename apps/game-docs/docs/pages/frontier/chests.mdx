# Chests

A chest opens the moment you earn it, where the army stands. There is nothing to carry home and nothing anyone can take.

## Two rolls

**What it is.** The same everywhere: nine chests in ten hold a relic, about one in eleven a cosmetic, and one in a
hundred a token claim. Deeper is better, not different.

**How good it is.** Set by the ground you were standing on.

| Ground       | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Epic |
| ------------ | ------ | -------- | ---- | ---- |
| Surface      | 78%    | 18%      | 3.5% | 0.5% |
| Ethereal I   | 60%    | 30%      | 8%   | 2%   |
| Ethereal II  | 42%    | 38%      | 15%  | 5%   |
| Ethereal III | 25%    | 43%      | 23%  | 9%   |

## What each kind does

- **Relics** stay on the army that found them and cost nothing to activate.
- **Cosmetics** go to your account, drawn from the ones you do not own yet.
- **Token claims** are recorded on your account for payout later; the payout itself is not part of this season.

No chest is ever a blank. Every one gives a relic, a cosmetic or a token claim.
