# Chests

Every Frontier chest pays LORDS, from the season's pool, into your realm. There are two: the chest a ruin guards, and
the rare chest a reveal turns up. A chest pays LORDS and nothing else: no item, relic or cosmetic
([LORDS](/account/lords)).

## A ruin's chest

Clearing a ruin pays its chest, and gives the army XP by the ruin guard's starting strength. The chest's rarity is
rolled when the ruin is found, from the odds of the depth it stands on:

LORDS. The Season board's chip shows the season's pool, what today releases and what a Common chest pays today. A ruin
shows its tier and LORDS before you fight. Its LORDS are reserved and paid only when you clear it. If today's available
budget cannot cover it, the clear refuses without spending the attack. You can reveal any number of ruins, then choose
one to clear: your other ruins disappear, across every depth, and your realm finds no more ruins until the next day.

## A chest in the wild

Each newly revealed tile at any depth has a 1 in {FRONTIER.discovery.exploreChestOneIn} chance of a chest, found only
while the day's budget can fund it. Its tier uses the layer's regular chest odds, with its shares at today's price set
aside as it is found. Open it with one of your armies beside it before the day ends; its LORDS go to that army's realm.

## The season's prizes

Part of the season's pool pays chests. The rest, the leaderboard part, is shared by the top of the Season board when the
season ends; each season announces its amounts. Only realms that have cleared at least one site take a place. The Season
page shows the podium as the season runs, with each place's LORDS and who holds it, and each prize as Pending until it
is paid.

## Your LORDS

Your realm's LORDS pay for Refill. During the season, **Withdraw** sends them to your payout wallet.

When the season ends, whatever your realm still holds, and any prize it won, is paid to your linked payout wallet.
Payments are automatic once the payout checks complete. With no wallet linked, link one within
{FRONTIER.claimWindow.days} days of the season's end, and they are paid to it. After that, unpaid LORDS return to the
treasury. Until a wallet is linked, the game says so wherever it shows your LORDS, with **Link wallet**
([Payout wallet](/account/payout-wallet)).
