# Realms: Frontier

Explore the unknown. Find the gold. Nobody can take your realm.

Frontier is a treasure hunt on a new map every day. You send armies out of your realm into the fog, fight what guards
the land, and bring home Essence, labor and LORDS. It is free to play: your realm is founded as you first enter, you
fight the wilds and never other players, and the map is new every day while your realm keeps what it earned.

## One day, one map

A day lasts {FRONTIER.day.shortestHours} to {FRONTIER.day.longestHours} hours. The lengths are drawn for the whole
season in bags of {FRONTIER.day.daysPerBag} days, one of each length, {FRONTIER.day.bagHours} hours a bag; no bag opens
with the shortest day, and two shortest days never follow each other. The top bar shows when today ends and how long
tomorrow lasts.

1. **Deploy.** Barracks train troops at your realm. Deploying puts them on a tile beside it as an army, at
   {FRONTIER.stamina.start} stamina. A new realm fields {FRONTIER.realm.armies[0]} armies at once; its castle raises
   that to {FRONTIER.realm.armies[3]}.
2. **Explore.** The map is hidden until you walk it. What sits on a tile is decided the moment an army reveals it, so
   nobody can scout ahead.
3. **Clear.** Trolls, rifts, ruins and beasts are guarded. A site shows its exact guard and its payout before you attack,
   and an attack needs {FRONTIER.stamina.attack} stamina.
4. **Bring it home.** Everything found goes straight to the realm. An army never carries anything worth losing.

When the day ends, the map and every army on it are gone. Your realm is raised again on the next map with everything it
holds.

## What carries over

| Gone when the day ends           | Kept by your realm                   |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| The map and its fog              | Its buildings, castle and research   |
| Every Troll, rift, ruin and beast | Its troops, wheat, labor and Essence |
| Every army you sent out          | Its LORDS                            |

An army with Homecoming sends some of its survivors home as the day ends ([The Expedition](/frontier/expedition)).

## Stamina sets the pace

An army holds {FRONTIER.stamina.max} stamina and recovers {FRONTIER.stamina.perTick} every{" "}
{FRONTIER.stamina.tickMinutes} minutes, so an empty bar fills in {FRONTIER.stamina.fullHours} hours. Revealing a tile
costs {FRONTIER.stamina.reveal}, a step over known ground {FRONTIER.stamina.step}, and an attack needs{" "}
{FRONTIER.stamina.attack}. Every step and every reveal also costs {FRONTIER.stamina.wheatPerTroop} wheat per troop, so a
big army needs farms behind it.

Refill tops an army's stamina up at once for {FRONTIER.stamina.refillLords} LORDS a missing point, paid from your
realm's LORDS. A well gives {FRONTIER.stamina.well} stamina, buying an attribute tier gives{" "}
{FRONTIER.stamina.tierBought}, and Logistics raises an army's maximum by up to {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsMax}.

## Fights roll dice

Each side of a fight rolls a d20 and hits up to 20% harder, so the attack preview shows a range from your worst roll to
your best. The ground does not matter: no biome helps or hurts either side.

## Your realm is never attacked

No player can raid, besiege or take it, and no player can fight your armies. The realm has no guard slots because it
needs none. Two of your armies standing side by side can merge, keeping the lower of their two staminas, and an army you
disband loses its troops; Frontier asks first.

## What you are given

Each Realm in your payout wallet gives your Frontier realm {FRONTIER.labor.perRealm} labor once a day: add it by its
number, then Claim all. Everything else is what you cleared and what you were lucky enough to walk into. Frontier has no
trade, market, bridge, guilds, faith, hyperstructures or automation.
