# Realms: Frontier

Explore the unknown. Find the gold. Nobody can take your realm.

Frontier is a treasure hunt in daily expeditions. You send armies out of your realm into the fog, fight what guards the
land, and bring home Essence and chests. It is free to play, you fight the wilds and never other players, and the map is
new every expedition while your realm keeps what it earned.

## One expedition, one day

An expedition runs for one day, from 00:00 UTC to the next. You commit troops in the morning and they do not come back:
a committed army is spent whether it dies or not. What comes back is Essence, and Essence is what makes your realm
stronger for tomorrow.

1. **Muster.** Troops leave the garrison as armies of 150 stamina on your home ring, paying wheat on every step. A new
   realm fields three armies at once; its castle raises that to four, five and six.
2. **Explore.** The map is hidden until you walk it. What sits on a tile is decided the moment an army reveals it, so
   nobody can scout ahead.
3. **Clear.** Camps, rifts and troves are guarded. A site shows its exact guard before you attack, an attack costs 50
   stamina, and winning refunds none of it.
4. **Bring it home.** Everything found goes straight to the realm. Chests open the moment you earn them.

At the end of the day the map and every army on it are gone. Your realm is raised again on the next map with everything
you built.

## What carries over

| Gone at the end of every expedition | Rebuilt with your realm, every time | Kept on your account    |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| The map and its fog                 | Castle, barracks, plots, the spire  | Chests and token claims |
| Camps, rifts and troves             | The garrison you did not commit     | Cosmetics               |
| Every army you sent out             | Stored wheat, labor and Essence     | Your record             |

## Stamina sets the pace

An army holds 150 stamina and recovers 30 an hour, so a full bar takes five hours. Revealing a tile costs 30, moving
over known ground 10, and an attack 50. The army panel shows how long until an army is rested.

## Fights roll dice

Each side of a fight rolls a d20 and hits 1% to 20% harder, so the attack preview shows a range from your worst roll to
your best. The ground does not matter: no biome helps or hurts either side.

## Your realm is never attacked

No player can raid, besiege or take it, and no player can fight your armies. The realm has no guard slots because it
needs none. Armies cannot be sent home, stationed or topped up from the garrison: deployed is deployed. Two of your
armies standing side by side can merge, and an army you disband loses its troops.

## Nothing is handed out

No daily gift, no consolation chest. What you bring home is what you cleared and what you were lucky enough to walk
into. Frontier has no trade, market, bridge, guilds, faith, hyperstructures or automation.
