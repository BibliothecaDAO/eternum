# Oaths and relics

Oaths and relics power up individual armies for the current game day. Both reset when the day ends.

## Oaths (from Shrines and bands)

<GameIcon name="shrine" /> Research **Shrine lore** at your castle to find Shrines ({FRONTIER.discovery.shrine} chance).
Step beside a Shrine and open it to draw 3 Oaths. Clearing a band on the Surface, Veil, or Hollow also draws 3 Oaths.

- Every army starts with {FRONTIER.oaths.baseSlots} Oath slots. Researching Oath slots at your castle raises this up to
  {FRONTIER.oaths.maxSlots} slots per troop kind ([Your realm](/frontier/realm#oath-slots)). When an army's slots are
  full, taking a new Oath replaces one you pick.
- Passing on a draw skips the Oaths and gives that army +{FRONTIER.oaths.passStamina} stamina.
- Rerolls: {FRONTIER.oaths.rerollNote} See [Shop and referrals](/frontier/shop-and-referrals) for current Shop status.

## Relics (from Ruins)

<GameIcon name="ruin" /> Clearing a Ruin gives a relic to the attacking army. An army can carry up to
{FRONTIER.relics.slots} relics at once; when full, you pick which slot to replace.

Relics come in {FRONTIER.relics.tiersCount} tiers ({FRONTIER.relics.firstTier} I to {FRONTIER.relics.lastTier} V), and
deeper layers drop higher tiers.

- {FRONTIER.relics.layerOdds[0].layer}: {FRONTIER.relics.layerOdds[0].odds}
- {FRONTIER.relics.layerOdds[1].layer}: {FRONTIER.relics.layerOdds[1].odds}
- {FRONTIER.relics.layerOdds[2].layer}: {FRONTIER.relics.layerOdds[2].odds}

An active relic lasts {FRONTIER.relics.minutesPerTier} minutes per tier, from {FRONTIER.relics.minutesPerTier} minutes
for {FRONTIER.relics.firstTier} I up to {FRONTIER.relics.maxMinutes} minutes for {FRONTIER.relics.lastTier} V.
