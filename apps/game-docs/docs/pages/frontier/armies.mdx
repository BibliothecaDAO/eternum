# Troops and stamina

Each day you deploy armies from your realm to explore, clear guarded sites, collect [Oaths](/frontier/oaths) and
[Relics](/frontier/relics), and fight in [the Deep](/frontier/deep). An army holds one troop kind and never mixes
troops. When the game day ends, deployed armies, their Oaths, and their relics end with the day; no survivors return
home, and your realm keeps the Oath slots it has researched.

## Deploying

Open **Deploy** to place troops on a tile beside your realm in an open army slot: {FRONTIER.realm.armies[0]} slots at
{FRONTIER.realm.castleLevelInline[0]}, up to {FRONTIER.realm.armies[3]} at {FRONTIER.realm.castleLevelInline[3]}. Each
troop costs {FRONTIER.realm.deployWheat} wheat to deploy (reduced by {FRONTIER.realm.rationStep} per Rations rank), and
each castle level raises the max troops per deploy ([Realm and research](/frontier/realm#the-castle)).

## Stamina

An army holds {FRONTIER.stamina.max} stamina and recovers {FRONTIER.stamina.perTick} every
{FRONTIER.stamina.tickMinutes} minutes ({FRONTIER.stamina.perHour} per hour), so an empty bar fills in
{FRONTIER.stamina.fullHours} hours. Revealing a tile costs {FRONTIER.stamina.reveal} for {FRONTIER.troops.knight} and
{FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman}, and {FRONTIER.stamina.scoutReveal} for {FRONTIER.troops.scout}. A step on an explored
tile costs {FRONTIER.stamina.step}, and an attack needs {FRONTIER.stamina.attack}. Every step and every reveal also
costs {FRONTIER.stamina.wheatPerTroop} wheat per troop, so a large army needs many farms.

- <GameIcon name={FRONTIER.buildingIcons.supplyYard} /> A {FRONTIER.names.supplyYard} gives new armies Logistics rank I,
  and research raises it to rank V. Each rank adds {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsStep} max stamina, adding
  {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsBonus} bonus stamina at rank V for {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsMax} max stamina total.
- <GameIcon name="well" /> Revealing a Well refills {FRONTIER.stamina.well} immediately ({FRONTIER.stamina.wellBase} on
  a {FRONTIER.stamina.max} bar, or {FRONTIER.stamina.wellLogisticsBase} at Logistics V). With the Reflection Oath, a
  Well refills {FRONTIER.oaths.reflectionWell} ({FRONTIER.stamina.wellReflection} on a {FRONTIER.stamina.max} bar, or
  {FRONTIER.stamina.wellLogisticsReflection} at Logistics V). Stamina above the bar maximum is lost.
- <GameIcon name="fragment" /> {FRONTIER.shop.staminaRule} See [Shop and referrals](/frontier/shop-and-referrals) for
  current Shop status.

### Stamina stays with the slot

Stamina stays on the army slot rather than on the troops inside it. Deploy picks your first open slot, or you can choose
another before deploying.

- A slot's first army of the day starts with a full bar ({FRONTIER.stamina.max} stamina, plus your Logistics bonus).
- When an army dies, disbands, or merges into another, its slot keeps the stamina it had left and keeps recovering.
- The next army deployed into that slot starts with that remaining stamina.
- When the day ends, every slot resets so tomorrow's first army starts full again.

## Merging, reinforcing, and disbanding

Two armies of the same troop kind standing side by side can merge, keeping the lower stamina and the receiving army's
Logistics rank. You can also reinforce an army beside your realm or beside any camp your realm cleared today (supply
post) on the {FRONTIER.layers.personalOr} using troops and wheat from home. Disbanding an army deletes its troops after
you confirm.
