# Armies

## Deploying

Barracks train Knights at your realm. From City, Scouts’ lodges train Scouts. Choose a troop kind in **Deploy** to put
them on a tile beside the realm as an army, in one of its army slots: {FRONTIER.realm.armies[0]} for a new realm, up to
{FRONTIER.realm.armies[3]} as its castle rises. Each troop deployed costs {FRONTIER.realm.deployWheat} wheat, and each
castle level deploys more troops at once ([Your realm](/frontier/realm#the-castle)).

## Stamina

An army holds {FRONTIER.stamina.max} stamina and recovers {FRONTIER.stamina.perTick} every
{FRONTIER.stamina.tickMinutes}{" "} minutes, so an empty bar fills in {FRONTIER.stamina.fullHours} hours. Revealing a
tile costs {FRONTIER.stamina.reveal} for Knights and {FRONTIER.stamina.scoutReveal} for Scouts, a step over known ground
{FRONTIER.stamina.step}, and an attack needs {FRONTIER.stamina.attack}. Every step and every reveal also costs
{FRONTIER.stamina.wheatPerTroop} wheat per troop, so a big army needs farms behind it.

Logistics training raises an army's maximum by up to {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsMax}.

### Stamina stays with the slot

Stamina belongs to the army slot, not to the army in it. Deploy picks your first open slot; choose another before
deploying if you like.

- A slot's first army of the day starts with a full bar: {FRONTIER.stamina.max} stamina, plus what your Supply yard's
  Logistics adds.
- When an army dies, is disbanded or merges into another, its slot keeps the stamina it had left, and that stamina keeps
  recovering.
- The next army deployed into that slot starts with that stamina, not a full bar. Replacing a tired army never brings a
  fresh one.
- The new army's own maximum still caps it. Its Logistics rank starts from your realm's training; only the stamina
  carries over.
- When the day ends, every slot is cleared, so each slot's first army tomorrow starts full again.

## Merging and disbanding

Two of your armies standing side by side can merge, keeping the lower of their two staminas. The army you merge into is
the one that stays; when the other has stronger Logistics training, Frontier asks once more before it is lost. An army
you disband loses its troops; Frontier asks first.

## Logistics training

A Supply yard grants new armies Logistics rank I. Research raises their starting rank through V. A full merge keeps the
receiving army's Logistics training.

## Fights

No dice are rolled in Frontier, so a fight's forecast is its result. Before you attack, a site's card shows each side's
troops before and after, and what clearing it pays. The ground does not matter: no biome helps or hurts either side.

Every enemy shows a blade or a bow. Bows hit first in every attack; only the surviving blades hit back. Two blades or
two bows strike together. Crossbows and Scouts deal {FRONTIER.attributes.lightDamageReduction} less damage than Knights.
A Knight army holding a guard slot takes {FRONTIER.attributes.knightGuardReduction} less damage; a blade beast has no
such protection.
