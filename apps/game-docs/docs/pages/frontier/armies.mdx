## Deploying

Open **Deploy** to place troops on a tile beside your realm in an open army slot: {FRONTIER.realm.armies[0]} slots at
castle level 1 ({FRONTIER.realm.castleNames[0]}), up to {FRONTIER.realm.armies[3]} at castle level 4
({FRONTIER.realm.castleNames[3]}). Each troop costs {FRONTIER.realm.deployWheat} wheat to deploy (reduced by
{FRONTIER.realm.rationStep} per Rations rank), and each castle level raises the max troops per deploy
([Your realm](/frontier/realm#the-castle)).

## Stamina

An army holds {FRONTIER.stamina.max} stamina and recovers {FRONTIER.stamina.perTick} every
{FRONTIER.stamina.tickMinutes} minutes, so an empty bar fills in {FRONTIER.stamina.fullHours} hours. Revealing a tile
costs {FRONTIER.stamina.reveal} for Knights and {FRONTIER.stamina.scoutReveal} for Scouts, a step on an explored tile
costs {FRONTIER.stamina.step}, and an attack needs {FRONTIER.stamina.attack}. Every step and every reveal also costs
{FRONTIER.stamina.wheatPerTroop} wheat per troop, so a large army needs many farms.

- <GameIcon name={FRONTIER.buildingIcons.supplyYard} /> A {FRONTIER.names.supplyYard} gives new armies Logistics rank I,
  and research raises it to rank V. Each rank adds {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsStep} max stamina, adding +
  {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsBonus} bonus stamina at rank V for {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsMax} max stamina total.
- <GameIcon name="well" /> Visiting a Well refills {FRONTIER.stamina.well} (or {FRONTIER.oaths.reflectionWell} with the
  Reflection Oath).
- <GameIcon name="fragment" /> {FRONTIER.shop.staminaRule} See [Shop and referrals](/frontier/shop-and-referrals) for
  current Shop status.

### Stamina stays with the slot

Stamina stays on the army slot rather than on the troops inside it. Deploy picks your first open slot, or you can choose
another before deploying.

- A slot's first army of the day starts with a full bar ({FRONTIER.stamina.max} stamina, plus your Logistics bonus).
- When an army dies, disbands, or merges into another, its slot keeps the stamina it had left and keeps recovering.
- The next army deployed into that slot starts with that remaining stamina.
- When the day ends, every slot resets so tomorrow's first army starts full again.

## Merging, reinforcing, and disbanding

Two armies of the same troop kind standing side by side can merge, keeping the lower stamina and the receiving army's
Logistics rank. You can also reinforce an army beside your realm or beside any cleared camp (supply post) using troops
and wheat from home. Disbanding an army deletes its troops after you confirm.

## Fights

Combat uses no dice, so every fight forecast is exact. Before you attack, the fight card shows both sides' troops before
and after, plus the reward. Terrain gives no combat bonus or penalty.

Every enemy carries a **blade** or a **bow**.

- Bows strike first against blades, and only surviving blades hit back.
- Two blades or two bows strike at the same time.
- Crossbowmen and Scouts deal {FRONTIER.attributes.lightDamageReduction} less damage than Knights.
- Knights holding a Deep guard slot take {FRONTIER.attributes.knightGuardReduction} less damage, and blade beasts do not
  get this bonus.

Power up your armies further with [Oaths and relics](/frontier/oaths-and-relics).
