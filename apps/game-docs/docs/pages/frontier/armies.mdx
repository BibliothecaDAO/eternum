# Armies

## Deploying

Barracks train Knights at your realm. **Deploy** puts them on a tile beside the realm as an army, in one of its army
slots: {FRONTIER.realm.armies[0]} for a new realm, up to {FRONTIER.realm.armies[3]} as its castle rises. Each troop
deployed costs {FRONTIER.realm.deployWheat} wheat, and each castle level deploys more troops at once
([Your realm](/frontier/realm#the-castle)).

## Stamina

An army holds {FRONTIER.stamina.max} stamina and recovers {FRONTIER.stamina.perTick} every
{FRONTIER.stamina.tickMinutes}{" "} minutes, so an empty bar fills in {FRONTIER.stamina.fullHours} hours. Revealing a
tile costs {FRONTIER.stamina.reveal}, a step over known ground {FRONTIER.stamina.step}, and an attack needs
{FRONTIER.stamina.attack}. Every step and every reveal also costs {FRONTIER.stamina.wheatPerTroop} wheat per troop, so a
big army needs farms behind it.

**Refill** tops an army's stamina up at once for {FRONTIER.stamina.refillLords} LORDS a missing point, paid from your
realm's LORDS. A well gives {FRONTIER.stamina.well} stamina, buying an attribute tier gives
{FRONTIER.stamina.tierBought}, and Logistics raises an army's maximum by up to {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsMax}.

### Stamina stays with the slot

Stamina belongs to the army slot, not to the army in it. A new army takes your realm's lowest free slot.

- A slot's first army of the day starts with a full bar: {FRONTIER.stamina.max} stamina, plus what your Supply yard's
  Logistics adds.
- When an army dies, is disbanded or merges into another, its slot keeps the stamina it had left, and that stamina keeps
  recovering.
- The next army deployed into that slot starts with that stamina, not a full bar. Replacing a tired army never brings a
  fresh one.
- The new army's own maximum still caps it. Its XP and tiers start again from your realm's training; only the stamina
  carries over.
- When the day ends, every slot is cleared, so each slot's first army tomorrow starts full again.

## Merging and disbanding

Two of your armies standing side by side can merge, keeping the lower of their two staminas. An army you disband loses
its troops; Frontier asks first.

## XP and attributes

An army earns {FRONTIER.xp.reveal} XP for each tile it reveals, XP for each guard it clears (more for a stronger guard),
and {FRONTIER.xp.shrine} XP from a shrine. It spends XP on the attribute tiers you choose, up to legendary:
{FRONTIER.xp.tierPrices[0]}, {FRONTIER.xp.tierPrices[1]}, {FRONTIER.xp.tierPrices[2]}, then
{FRONTIER.xp.tierPrices[3]}{" "} XP for each next tier.

- **Battle** hits up to {FRONTIER.attributes.battleMax} harder.
- **Logistics** carries up to {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsMax} more stamina.
- **Scouting** adds {FRONTIER.attributes.scouting[0]}, {FRONTIER.attributes.scouting[1]},
  {FRONTIER.attributes.scouting[2]}, then {FRONTIER.attributes.scouting[3]} to the find rate of the camps, rifts or
  beasts you pick for each tier, so all four on one kind add {FRONTIER.attributes.scoutingOneKind}.
- **Homecoming** sends survivors home when the day ends ([The expedition](/frontier/expedition#the-days-end)).

An army's XP and tiers end with it. Your realm's training buildings start every new army a tier higher
([Your realm](/frontier/realm#research)).

## Fights

No dice are rolled in Frontier, so a fight's forecast is its result. Before you attack, a site's card shows each side's
troops before and after, and what clearing it pays. The ground does not matter: no biome helps or hurts either side.
