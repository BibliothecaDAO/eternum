# Relics

<GameIcon name="ruin" /> Relics give an army a timed bonus you activate when you need it. Clearing a ruin that does not
hold a Fragment cache gives 1 relic to the army that lands the last hit.

## How relics work

- **Slots:** an army carries up to {FRONTIER.relics.slots} relics at once. When full, winning another relic lets you
  pick which slot to replace.
- **Activation:** activating a relic is free and works anywhere, including inside [the Deep](/frontier/deep). An army
  can have one active effect per relic kind at a time.
- **Duration:** an active relic lasts {FRONTIER.relics.minutesPerTier} minutes per tier, from
  {FRONTIER.relics.minutesPerTier} minutes for {FRONTIER.relics.firstTier} (I) up to {FRONTIER.relics.maxMinutes}
  minutes for {FRONTIER.relics.lastTier} (V).
- **Bound to the army:** relics cannot be sold, traded, or passed to another army (even when merging), and end when the
  army ends with the day.

Relics come in {FRONTIER.relics.tiersCount} tiers ({FRONTIER.relics.firstTier} through {FRONTIER.relics.lastTier}), and
