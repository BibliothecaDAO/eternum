# Quests and banes

Every game day brings a **daily quest** for your realm. On some days, a **World Bane** or a **rival Team Bane** replaces
today's quest with a bane quest that your whole Aspect clears together.

## Daily quests

Complete today's objective and press **Collect** to claim your reward.

- A castle level 1 realm receives {FRONTIER.quest.labor} Labor and {FRONTIER.quest.essence} Essence.
- Rewards multiply by {FRONTIER.quest.levelStep} at each castle level ({FRONTIER.quest.levelScale}).
- A held Realm or an Ancient Seal boosts daily quest labor to {FRONTIER.quest.bonusPercent}.
- A held Wonder Realm also grants +{FRONTIER.quest.wonderFragments} Fragments when you collect.

The {FRONTIER.quest.count} daily quests are dealt in shuffled {FRONTIER.quest.count}-day bags so the same quest never
repeats back to back:

| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **{FRONTIER.quest.list[0].name}** | {FRONTIER.quest.list[0].objective} | {FRONTIER.quest.list[0].baseCount} |
| **{FRONTIER.quest.list[1].name}** | {FRONTIER.quest.list[1].objective} | {FRONTIER.quest.list[1].baseCount} |
| **{FRONTIER.quest.list[2].name}** | {FRONTIER.quest.list[2].objective} | {FRONTIER.quest.list[2].baseCount} |
| **{FRONTIER.quest.list[3].name}** | {FRONTIER.quest.list[3].objective} | {FRONTIER.quest.list[3].baseCount} |

## World Banes

World Banes strike the whole world on random days. Each peaceful day adds +{FRONTIER.banes.chanceStep} to the chance of
a World Bane on the next day.

| World Bane                                                                          | Effect while active              | Bane quest to break it                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <GameIcon name={FRONTIER.banes.world[0].icon} /> **{FRONTIER.banes.world[0].name}** | {FRONTIER.banes.world[0].effect} | **{FRONTIER.banes.world[0].questName}:** {FRONTIER.banes.world[0].questObjective} |
| <GameIcon name={FRONTIER.banes.world[1].icon} /> **{FRONTIER.banes.world[1].name}** | {FRONTIER.banes.world[1].effect} | **{FRONTIER.banes.world[1].questName}:** {FRONTIER.banes.world[1].questObjective} |
| <GameIcon name={FRONTIER.banes.world[2].icon} /> **{FRONTIER.banes.world[2].name}** | {FRONTIER.banes.world[2].effect} | **{FRONTIER.banes.world[2].questName}:** {FRONTIER.banes.world[2].questObjective} |

Each Aspect tracks its own progress. Once {FRONTIER.banes.quorum} of your Aspect's active members finish the bane quest,
the bane ends for your Aspect, and everyone who finished can press **Collect again** for a second daily quest payout.

## Rival Team Banes

<GameIcon name="hyperstructure" /> When a rival Aspect fills its **Bane altar** bar ({FRONTIER.deep.altarThreshold}
charge) in [the Deep](/frontier/deep), a **Team Bane** hits the leading rival Aspect on the next game day.

- The targeted Aspect's hyperstructure stops gaining height while the Team Bane lasts.
- The targeted Aspect gets a bane quest ({FRONTIER.banes.questNames}). Once {FRONTIER.banes.quorum} of its members
  finish the quest, the Team Bane lifts and height growth resumes.
