# Quests and banes

Every game day brings a **daily quest** for your realm. On some days, a **World Bane** or a **rival Team Bane** replaces
today's quest with a bane quest that your whole Aspect clears together.

## Daily quests

Complete today's objective and press **Collect** to claim your reward.

- A castle level 1 ({FRONTIER.realm.castleNames[0]}) realm receives {FRONTIER.quest.labor} Labor and
  {FRONTIER.quest.essence} Essence.
- Rewards multiply by {FRONTIER.quest.levelStep} at each castle level ({FRONTIER.quest.levelScale}).
- A **Realm NFT** or an Ancient Seal boosts daily quest labor to {FRONTIER.quest.bonusPercent}.
- A **Wonder Realm NFT** also grants +{FRONTIER.quest.wonderFragments} Fragments when you collect.

Each of the {FRONTIER.quest.count} daily quests comes once every {FRONTIER.quest.count} days in shuffled order, and the
same quest never runs two days in a row:

| **{FRONTIER.quest.list[0].name}** | {FRONTIER.quest.list[0].objective} | {FRONTIER.quest.list[0].baseCount} |
| **{FRONTIER.quest.list[1].name}** | {FRONTIER.quest.list[1].objective} | {FRONTIER.quest.list[1].baseCount} |
| **{FRONTIER.quest.list[2].name}** | {FRONTIER.quest.list[2].objective} | {FRONTIER.quest.list[2].baseCount} |

## World Banes

World Banes strike the whole world on random days. Each day without a World Bane adds +{FRONTIER.banes.chanceStep} to
the chance of one on the next day. Once any Aspect issues the season's first Team Bane, World Banes stop for the rest of
the season.

| World Bane                                                                          | Effect while active              | Bane quest to break it                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <GameIcon name={FRONTIER.banes.world[0].icon} /> **{FRONTIER.banes.world[0].name}** | {FRONTIER.banes.world[0].effect} | **{FRONTIER.banes.world[0].questName}:** {FRONTIER.banes.world[0].questObjective} |
| <GameIcon name={FRONTIER.banes.world[1].icon} /> **{FRONTIER.banes.world[1].name}** | {FRONTIER.banes.world[1].effect} | **{FRONTIER.banes.world[1].questName}:** {FRONTIER.banes.world[1].questObjective} |
| <GameIcon name={FRONTIER.banes.world[2].icon} /> **{FRONTIER.banes.world[2].name}** | {FRONTIER.banes.world[2].effect} | **{FRONTIER.banes.world[2].questName}:** {FRONTIER.banes.world[2].questObjective} |

Each Aspect tracks its own progress. Once {FRONTIER.banes.quorum} of your Aspect's members who were active on the
previous day finish the bane quest, the bane ends for your Aspect, and everyone who finished can press **Collect again**
for a second daily quest payout.

## Rival Team Banes

<GameIcon name="hyperstructure" /> When an Aspect fills its **Bane altar** bar ({FRONTIER.deep.altarThreshold} charge)
in [the Deep](/frontier/deep), a **Team Bane** hits the rival Aspect with the tallest hyperstructure on the next game
day, skipping any rival already blocked or already targeted for tomorrow. No Team Bane is issued on the last day of the
season.

- The targeted Aspect's hyperstructure stops gaining height while the Team Bane lasts.
- The targeted Aspect gets a bane quest ({FRONTIER.banes.questNames}). Once {FRONTIER.banes.quorum} of its members who
  were active on the previous day finish the quest, the Team Bane lifts and height growth resumes.
