# Daily quests

Every game day brings a **daily quest** for your realm. Every realm can complete every daily quest from day 1 without
any lore, building, or layer unlock. Complete today's objective and press **Collect** for your reward.

- Each of the {FRONTIER.quest.count} daily quests comes once every {FRONTIER.quest.count} days in shuffled order, and
  the same quest never runs two days in a row.
- The target count is the base target multiplied by your castle level (1x at {FRONTIER.realm.castleLevelInline[0]}, up
  to {FRONTIER.realm.maxLevel}x at {FRONTIER.realm.castleLevelInline[3]}).
- Rewards start at {FRONTIER.quest.labor} labor and {FRONTIER.quest.essence} Essence at
  {FRONTIER.realm.castleLevelInline[0]} and multiply by {FRONTIER.quest.levelStep} at each castle level
  ({FRONTIER.quest.levelScale}).
- A **Realm NFT** (a Realms collection NFT held in your linked payout wallet) or an Ancient Seal boosts daily quest
  labor to {FRONTIER.quest.bonusPercent}.
- A **Wonder Realm NFT** also grants +{FRONTIER.quest.wonderFragments} Fragments when you collect.

On World Bane or rival Team Bane days, a bane quest replaces today's daily quest
([Banes and Triumphs](/frontier/banes)).
