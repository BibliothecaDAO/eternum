# Devcon season

A Frontier season at Devcon speed, with {DEVCON.pool} LORDS to win. It runs from **{DEVCON.starts.ist}**
({DEVCON.starts.utc}) to **{DEVCON.ends.ist}** ({DEVCON.ends.utc}). It plays like any Frontier season
([Frontier](/frontier)), only faster.

## Join with your ticket

1. **Scan your flyer's QR code, or open a friend's invite link.** It opens your sealed Devcon ticket.
2. **Open your ticket.** Sign in with Discord or your email ([Sign in](/account/sign-in)). Your first sign-in creates
   your Realms account. The ticket then shows whether it is regular or golden, and what its realm claims each day.
3. **Start your adventure.** The Devcon game's Realm step, **Choose your home**, opens with your ticket chosen. Press
   **Create your realm**.

A ticket comes from a flyer, the shared flyer code or a friend's invite link. Each account holds one Devcon ticket.
Scanning a second flyer with an account that already holds one shows the ticket you have, and that flyer stays unspent
for someone else. Without a flyer you can still play: found your home with a Realm your payout wallet holds, or take a
free home.

## What your home claims

Your home claims Labor and Essence once every game day: press **Realm Labor** beside your LORDS (on a phone, in
**Menu**), then **Claim**. A day you miss is lost, and Labor that does not fit in your storage is lost with the claim.

| Home             | Labor                       | Essence                       |
| ---------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Devcon ticket    | {DEVCON.claim.ticket.labor} | {DEVCON.claim.ticket.essence} |
| Golden ticket    | {DEVCON.claim.golden.labor} | {DEVCON.claim.golden.essence} |
| A Realm you hold | {DEVCON.claim.realm}        | None                          |
| A free home      | None                        | None                          |

Every flyer looks the same. Some hold a golden ticket, revealed only when the ticket is opened. Friends can turn a
ticket golden too ([Invite friends](#invite-friends)). Devcon and golden realms have their own look on the map and on
the Season board.

## Invite friends

On **You**, share your invite link. Each friend who signs up through it gets a Devcon ticket, and 1 in
{DEVCON.goldenOneIn} of those tickets is golden.

A friend counts once they found a realm and clear a site. One person counts once: a friend who signs in with the same
email inbox or on the same device as you, or as a friend already counted, does not count. What counted friends give you
depends on your home:

| Your home                | What counted friends give you                                                             |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A free home, or none yet | A Devcon ticket at the first; golden instead if that friend's ticket is golden            |
| A Devcon home            | Golden, from your next daily claim once the game confirms it, when a golden friend counts |
| A golden home            | Nothing more                                                                              |
| A held Realm             | Nothing; your friends still get their tickets                                             |

At {DEVCON.goldenLinkAt} counted friends your link goes golden: you, every friend who used it, before and after, and
every ticket it gives from then on. A ticket your friends earn is added to a free home when you next enter the game:
**Choose a daily claim**, then **Add this daily claim**. A friend counts once, for the first link they used, and your
own link gives you nothing. Friends count until the season ends, {DEVCON.ends.ist} ({DEVCON.ends.utc}).

## The LORDS

The season's pool is {DEVCON.pool} LORDS:

- **{DEVCON.chestShare} pays chests:** {DEVCON.chestPool} LORDS, released day by day and paid by the ruins you clear and
  the chests your armies find ([Chests and LORDS](/frontier/chests)).
- **{DEVCON.leaderboardShare} pays the leaderboard:** {DEVCON.leaderboardPool} LORDS to the top {DEVCON.places} realms
  on the Season board at the season's end: {DEVCON.prizes[0]} for first, {DEVCON.prizes[1]} for second and
  {DEVCON.prizes[2]} for third. Only realms that cleared at least one site take a place.

The Season page shows the podium as the season runs, and each prize as Pending until it is paid.

LORDS go to your payout wallet. Link one in **Settings**, **Payout wallet** ([Payout wallet](/account/payout-wallet)).
You can withdraw your realm's LORDS during the season. When it ends, whatever your realm still holds, and your prize, is
paid to your linked wallet. Payments are automatic once the payout checks complete. Without a linked wallet, link one
within{" "} {FRONTIER.claimWindow.days} days of the season's end and they are paid to it. After that, unpaid LORDS
return to the treasury.

## What is faster

- A normal game day lasts {DEVCON.day.shortestHours} to {DEVCON.day.longestHours} hours ({FRONTIER.day.shortestHours}
  to{" "} {FRONTIER.day.longestHours} in a regular season). The final day stops at the season’s end; a last stub shorter
  than {DEVCON.day.shortestHours} hours joins the preceding day.
- Stamina recovers, and your buildings produce, {DEVCON.speed} times as fast.
- The Ethereal depths cost {DEVCON.depths[0]}, {DEVCON.depths[1]} and {DEVCON.depths[2]} Essence
  ({FRONTIER.research.depths[0].essence}, {FRONTIER.research.depths[1].essence} and{" "}
  {FRONTIER.research.depths[2].essence} in a regular season).
- The leaderboard pays its top {DEVCON.places}, with the prizes above.

Everything else is as the Frontier pages describe it.
