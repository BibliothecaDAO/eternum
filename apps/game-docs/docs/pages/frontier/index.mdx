# Frontier

Frontier is free to play at <PlayLink />. Each game day opens a fresh map around your realm: upgrade your town, deploy
armies into the fog for Oaths and relics, and fight in the Deep to raise your Aspect's hyperstructure for LORDS.

## The clock and a game day

Every army shares one clock:

- **Army tick:** {FRONTIER.stamina.tickMinutes} minutes. Every tick gives +{FRONTIER.stamina.perTick} stamina
  ({FRONTIER.stamina.perMinute}, filling a {FRONTIER.stamina.max}-stamina bar in {FRONTIER.stamina.fullHours} hours).
  Inside the Deep, each tick also removes {FRONTIER.deep.lossPerTick} troops per army and pays held sites.
- **Day lengths:** every {FRONTIER.day.daysPerBag}-day cycle holds one day each of {FRONTIER.day.lengths} hours
  ({FRONTIER.day.bagHours} hours total, {FRONTIER.day.averageHours} hours on average) in shuffled order. No
  {FRONTIER.day.daysPerBag}-day cycle starts with an {FRONTIER.day.shortestHours}-hour day, and two
  {FRONTIER.day.shortestHours}-hour days never run in a row. An {FRONTIER.day.shortestHours}-hour day gives an army
  {FRONTIER.stamina.day8h} total stamina; a {FRONTIER.day.longestHours}-hour day gives {FRONTIER.stamina.day24h}.
- **Season length:** {FRONTIER.day.seasonDays} game days at most (about {FRONTIER.day.seasonRealDays} real days). The
  season ends earlier as soon as one Aspect finishes its hyperstructure.

Each day follows four steps:

1. **<GameIcon name="deploy" /> Grow and deploy.** Upgrade your castle and buildings, then deploy
   {FRONTIER.troops.knight}, {FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman}, and {FRONTIER.troops.scout} on tiles beside your realm.
2. **<GameIcon name="explore" /> Explore.** Reveal hidden tiles on the {FRONTIER.layers.personalAnd}. Each reveal sends
   Essence or labor straight home and can uncover beasts, camps, rifts, ruins, shrines, wells, or your Spire.
3. **<GameIcon name="attack" /> Build today's armies.** Clear bands and reveal shrines to take Oaths, clear ruins for
   relics or Fragment caches, and finish today's daily quest or bane quest.
4. **<GameIcon name="hyperstructure" /> Enter the Deep.** Descend through your Spire into the shared Deep to hold sites,
   earn Glory, and raise your Aspect's hyperstructure toward {FRONTIER.race.finishHeight} height.

When a day ends, the map and every deployed army clear. Your realm appears again on the next day's map and keeps
everything it owns.

| Cleared at day's end                            | Kept for the season                              |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| The map and fog                                 | Your castle, buildings, research, and Oath slots |
| Camps, rifts, ruins, shrines, wells, and beasts | Troops, wheat, labor, and Essence at home        |
| Deployed armies, their Oaths, and active relics | Your Aspect, Order, Glory, Fragments, and Seal   |

## Your first minute

Entry is free and stays open all season, so players who join later start with the same resources. A new realm begins
with {FRONTIER.realm.troops} {FRONTIER.troops.knight} waiting at home, {FRONTIER.realm.wheat} wheat, and
{FRONTIER.realm.labor} labor. Your starting labor buys one of each starter building right away:
{FRONTIER.realm.firstMinuteSummary}. Build on your open plots, then deploy your first army into the fog.

New panels appear as your realm unlocks what they use. Open **Settings** and turn on **Show everything** under
**Interface** to show every panel right away.

## Guides

Follow the guides in the order you play:

1. **Global:** [Sign in](/account/sign-in), [Payout wallet](/account/payout-wallet), and [LORDS](/account/lords).
2. **Your realm:** [Realm and research](/frontier/realm), [Buildings](/frontier/buildings) (all
   {FRONTIER.buildingsCount} buildings and repeat copy prices), and [Daily quests](/frontier/quests).
3. **Armies:** [Troops and stamina](/frontier/armies), [Combat](/frontier/combat) (formula, blade vs bow, worked
   examples), [Oaths](/frontier/oaths) (picks, slots, all {FRONTIER.oaths.count} Oaths, strong pairs), and
   [Relics](/frontier/relics) (all {FRONTIER.relics.count} relic kinds).
4. **Exploring:** [The expedition](/frontier/expedition) (reveal odds, caches, supply posts, Spires) and
   [Beasts](/frontier/beasts) (all {FRONTIER.beastFamiliesCount} beast families and guard sizes by layer).
5. **The Deep:** [The Deep](/frontier/deep) (rings, {FRONTIER.deep.sitesCount} site kinds, guard slots, PvP) and
   [Banes and Triumphs](/frontier/banes) (World Banes, Team Banes, Stage Triumphs).
6. **The season:** [Aspects and rewards](/frontier/aspects) (the {FRONTIER.aspectsCount} Aspects, hyperstructure race,
   Glory, and LORDS payouts).
7. **Shop and friends:** [Shop and referrals](/frontier/shop-and-referrals) (Fragments, Ancient Seal, free caches, and
   referrals).
