# Frontier

Frontier is free to play. Every game day opens a fresh map around your realm. You build up your town, send armies into
the mist to clear beasts and sites, take Oaths and relics for the day, and enter the Deep to raise your Aspect's
hyperstructure for LORDS rewards.

## The clock and a game day

Every army shares one clock:

- **Army tick:** {FRONTIER.stamina.tickMinutes} minutes. Every tick gives +{FRONTIER.stamina.perTick} stamina
  ({FRONTIER.stamina.perHour} per hour, filling a {FRONTIER.stamina.max}-stamina bar in {FRONTIER.stamina.fullHours}
  hours). Inside the Deep, each tick also removes {FRONTIER.deep.lossPerTick} troops per army and pays held sites.
- **Day lengths:** every {FRONTIER.day.daysPerBag}-day bag holds one day each of {FRONTIER.day.lengths} hours
  ({FRONTIER.day.bagHours} hours total, {FRONTIER.day.averageHours} hours on average) in shuffled order. No bag starts
  with an {FRONTIER.day.shortestHours}-hour day, and two {FRONTIER.day.shortestHours}-hour days never run in a row. An
  {FRONTIER.day.shortestHours}-hour day gives an army {FRONTIER.stamina.day8h} total stamina; a
  {FRONTIER.day.longestHours}-hour day gives {FRONTIER.stamina.day24h}.
- **Season length:** {FRONTIER.day.seasonDays} game days at most ({FRONTIER.day.bagsPerSeason} bags, about
  {FRONTIER.day.seasonRealDays} real days). The season ends earlier as soon as one Aspect finishes its hyperstructure.

Each day follows four steps:

1. **<GameIcon name="deploy" /> Grow and deploy.** Upgrade your castle and buildings, then deploy
   {FRONTIER.troops.knight}, {FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman}, and {FRONTIER.troops.scout} on tiles beside your realm.
2. **<GameIcon name="explore" /> Explore.** Reveal hidden tiles on the {FRONTIER.layers.personalAnd}. Each reveal sends
   Essence or labor straight home and can uncover beasts, camps, rifts, ruins, shrines, wells, or your Spire.
3. **<GameIcon name="attack" /> Build today's armies.** Clear bands and reveal shrines to take Oaths, clear ruins for
   relics or Fragment caches, and finish today's daily quest or bane quest.
4. **<GameIcon name="hyperstructure" /> Enter the Deep.** Descend through your Spire into the shared Deep to hold sites,
   earn Glory, and push your Aspect's hyperstructure toward {FRONTIER.race.finishHeight} height.

When a day ends, the map and every deployed army clear. Your realm appears again on the next day's map and keeps
everything it owns.

| Cleared at day's end                            | Kept for the season                              |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| The map and fog                                 | Your castle, buildings, research, and Oath slots |
| Camps, rifts, ruins, shrines, wells, and beasts | Troops, wheat, labor, and Essence at home        |
| Deployed armies, their Oaths, and active relics | Your Aspect, Order, Glory, Fragments, and Seal   |

## Your first minute

Entry is free and stays open all season, so players who join later start with the same resources. A new realm begins
with {FRONTIER.realm.troops} {FRONTIER.troops.knight} waiting at home, {FRONTIER.realm.wheat} wheat, and
{FRONTIER.realm.labor} labor. Your starting labor buys one of each starter building right away:
{FRONTIER.realm.firstMinuteSummary}. Build on your open plots, then deploy your first army into the fog.
