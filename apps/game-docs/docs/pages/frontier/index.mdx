# Frontier

Frontier is free to play. Every game day a fresh map opens around your realm. You train armies, explore the fog, clear
guarded sites, and descend into the Deep to fight for your Aspect's hyperstructure.

## How a game day works

1. **<GameIcon name="deploy" /> Deploy.** Build barracks, ranges, and lodges at your realm, then place armies on tiles
   beside it.
2. **<GameIcon name="explore" /> Explore.** Reveal hidden tiles on the Surface, Veil, and Hollow. The game rolls what
   stands on a tile when your army reveals it, so you cannot scout ahead. Each reveal sends Essence or labor straight
   home.
3. **<GameIcon name="attack" /> Clear.** Fight roaming beasts, camps, rifts, and ruins. Every fight card shows the guard
   and the exact outcome before you attack.
4. **<GameIcon name="hyperstructure" /> Fight in the Deep.** Research Spire lore and send your strongest armies into the
   shared Deep to hold sites, earn Glory, and raise your Aspect's hyperstructure.

A normal day lasts {FRONTIER.day.shortestHours} to {FRONTIER.day.longestHours} hours. Lengths are drawn in bags of{" "}
{FRONTIER.day.daysPerBag} days, one of each length, totaling {FRONTIER.day.bagHours} hours per bag. No bag starts with
the shortest day, and two shortest days never run back to back. The top bar shows when today ends and how long tomorrow
lasts. The final day stops at the season's end, and any stub shorter than {FRONTIER.day.shortestHours} hours merges into
the day before it.

When a day ends, the game clears the map and all deployed armies. Your realm rises on the next day's map with everything
it owns.

| Cleared at day's end                            | Kept for the season                             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| The map and fog                                 | Your castle, buildings, and research            |
| Camps, rifts, ruins, shrines, wells, and beasts | Troops, wheat, labor, and Essence at home       |
| Deployed armies, their Oaths, and active relics | Your Aspect, Glory, Fragments, and Ancient Seal |

## What you can do

- Create your realm for free on any day of the season, with a Realm your payout wallet holds or a free home, and join
  one of the {FRONTIER.aspectsCount} Aspects.
- Build farms, workshops, storerooms, barracks, ranges, Scouts' lodges, a Conscription camp, a Rations depot, and a
  Supply yard, and raise your castle to Empire.
- Field Knights, Crossbowmen, and Scouts across {FRONTIER.realm.armies[0]} to {FRONTIER.realm.armies[3]} armies.
- Take Oaths at shrines and equip relics from ruins to power up each army for the day.
- Complete your daily quest and work with your Aspect to break World Banes and rival Team Banes.
- Enter the Deep to capture shared sites, earn Glory, and race to build your Aspect's hyperstructure for LORDS rewards.
- Visit another player's realm from the Season board.

## What you can't do

- Attack another player's home realm or armies on the Surface, Veil, or Hollow. Player fights happen only in the Deep.
- Trade or send resources to another player.
- Keep your deployed armies, Oaths, or relics after a game day ends.
- Win or lose a fight by luck. Combat uses no dice, so the fight forecast is exact.

## Your first day

A new realm starts with {FRONTIER.realm.troops} troops, {FRONTIER.realm.wheat} wheat, and {FRONTIER.realm.labor} labor.
Entry is free and stays open all season, so latecomers start on the same footing as day-one players. Build a barracks
and send your first army into the fog.
