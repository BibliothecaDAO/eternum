# Banes and Triumphs

Some game days bring a special day for your Aspect: a **World Bane** from the fog, a **Team Bane** sent by a rival
Aspect from [the Deep](/frontier/deep), or a **Stage Triumph** earned by raising your Aspect's hyperstructure. On bane
days, a bane quest replaces your normal [daily quest](/frontier/quests). A bane lasts at most that one game day.

## World Banes

The first {FRONTIER.banes.quietDays} game days never have a World Bane. From day {FRONTIER.banes.firstDay} onward, World
Banes can start for all {FRONTIER.aspectsCount} Aspects at the start of a game day. Each day without a World Bane, the
first {FRONTIER.banes.quietDays} days included, adds +{FRONTIER.banes.chanceStep} to the chance of one on the next day.
The chance is 0% the day after a World Bane. Once any Aspect issues the season's first Team Bane, World Banes stop for
the rest of the season. An Aspect never has a World Bane and a Team Bane on the same day.

Each player receives a personal bane quest — there is no single shared quest, and progress counts on every layer. Only
players who finish their own quest and press **Collect** that same day receive the daily quest reward. Once
{FRONTIER.banes.quorum} of your Aspect's members who deployed at least one army on the previous game day finish theirs,
the World Bane ends for your Aspect and finishers can press **Collect again** for a second equal reward (World Banes
only, not Team Banes). Players who did not finish receive no reward, only the bane's penalties removed.

## Rival Team Banes

<GameIcon name="hyperstructure" /> When an Aspect fills its **Bane altar** bar ({FRONTIER.deep.altarThreshold} charge)
in [the Deep](/frontier/deep), a **Team Bane** targets the rival Aspect with the tallest hyperstructure on the next game
day, skipping any rival already blocked or already targeted for tomorrow. No Team Bane is issued on the last day of the
season.

- The targeted Aspect's hyperstructure stops gaining height (and guards gain no build contribution) while the Team Bane
  lasts, and missed height is not returned when the bane ends. **Archive guards still earn their full Glory**
  ({FRONTIER.deep.payAlone} / {FRONTIER.deep.payPair} / {FRONTIER.deep.payCrowd} per tick, split by troop count) during
  a Team Bane.
- The targeted Aspect gets one of the {FRONTIER.banes.count} bane quests ({FRONTIER.banes.questNames}), never the same
  quest two times in a row.
- Finishing your own bane quest and pressing **Collect** that same day pays the daily quest reward. Once
  {FRONTIER.banes.quorum} of the Aspect's members who deployed at least one army on the previous game day finish theirs,
  the Team Bane ends and height growth resumes (with no second quest reward).

## Stage Triumphs

<GameIcon name="star" /> Completing hyperstructure stages 1 through 4 activates a **Triumph** for every member of your
Aspect on the next game day ([Aspects and rewards](/frontier/aspects)). Each Triumph lasts for that one game day only,
applies above the Deep only (Deep beasts, sites, and troop drain are unchanged), and no Triumph is scheduled on the last
day of the season. A Triumph and a World Bane on the same day multiply together.
