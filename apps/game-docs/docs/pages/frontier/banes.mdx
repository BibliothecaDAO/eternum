# Banes and Triumphs

Some game days bring a special day for your Aspect: a **World Bane** from the mist, a **Team Bane** sent by a rival
Aspect from [the Deep](/frontier/deep), or a **Stage Triumph** earned by raising your Aspect's hyperstructure. On bane
days, a bane quest replaces your normal [daily quest](/frontier/quests).

## World Banes

From day 2 onward, World Banes can strike all {FRONTIER.aspectsCount} Aspects at the start of a game day. The chance is
0% the day after a World Bane, and each day without a World Bane adds +{FRONTIER.banes.chanceStep} to the chance of one
on the next day. Once any Aspect issues the season's first Team Bane, World Banes stop for the rest of the season. An
Aspect never has a World Bane and a Team Bane on the same day.

Each Aspect tracks its own progress, and progress counts on every layer. Finishing the bane quest pays your normal daily
quest reward. Once {FRONTIER.banes.quorum} of your Aspect's members who were active on the previous day finish the bane
quest, the bane ends for your Aspect, and everyone who finished can press **Collect again** for a second daily quest
payout.

## Rival Team Banes

<GameIcon name="hyperstructure" /> When an Aspect fills its **Bane altar** bar ({FRONTIER.deep.altarThreshold} charge)
in [the Deep](/frontier/deep), a **Team Bane** hits the rival Aspect with the tallest hyperstructure on the next game
day, skipping any rival already blocked or already targeted for tomorrow. No Team Bane is issued on the last day of the
season.

- The targeted Aspect's hyperstructure stops gaining height while the Team Bane lasts. Height missed while blocked is
  not returned when the bane lifts.
- The targeted Aspect gets one of the {FRONTIER.banes.count} bane quests ({FRONTIER.banes.questNames}), never the same
  quest two times in a row.
- Finishing the bane quest pays the daily quest reward. Once {FRONTIER.banes.quorum} of the Aspect's members who were
  active on the previous day finish the quest, the Team Bane lifts and height growth resumes.

## Stage Triumphs

<GameIcon name="star" /> Completing hyperstructure stages 1 through 4 activates a **Triumph** for every member of your
Aspect on the next game day ([Aspects and rewards](/frontier/aspects)). Each Triumph lasts for that one game day only,
applies above the Deep only (Deep beasts, sites, and troop drain are unchanged), and no Triumph is scheduled on the last
day of the season. A Triumph and a World Bane on the same day multiply together.
