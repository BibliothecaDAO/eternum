# Your realm

Your realm makes the troops, wheat and labor your armies need, and grows stronger with research. A new realm starts with
{FRONTIER.realm.troops} troops, {FRONTIER.realm.wheat} wheat and {FRONTIER.realm.labor} labor.

## Buildings

Tap an open plot to build there. Buildings are paid in labor:

| Building                     | Makes                         | Labor                         | Population                         |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| {FRONTIER.buildings[0].name} | {FRONTIER.buildings[0].makes} | {FRONTIER.buildings[0].labor} | {FRONTIER.buildings[0].population} |
| {FRONTIER.buildings[1].name} | {FRONTIER.buildings[1].makes} | {FRONTIER.buildings[1].labor} | {FRONTIER.buildings[1].population} |
| {FRONTIER.buildings[2].name} | {FRONTIER.buildings[2].makes} | {FRONTIER.buildings[2].labor} | {FRONTIER.buildings[2].population} |
| {FRONTIER.buildings[3].name} | {FRONTIER.buildings[3].makes} | {FRONTIER.buildings[3].labor} | {FRONTIER.buildings[3].population} |
| {FRONTIER.buildings[4].name} | {FRONTIER.buildings[4].makes} | {FRONTIER.buildings[4].labor} | {FRONTIER.buildings[4].population} |

Each workers' hut houses {FRONTIER.realm.hutHouses}, and every other building takes the population shown. Each further
copy of a building costs more than the last, and pulling one down returns {FRONTIER.realm.demolitionRefund} of its
price. Barracks train for free; deploying costs {FRONTIER.realm.deployWheat} wheat a troop.

## The castle

Raise the castle for {FRONTIER.realm.castleLabor[0]}, {FRONTIER.realm.castleLabor[1]}, then{" "}
{FRONTIER.realm.castleLabor[2]} labor. Each level opens another ring of plots, fields one more army and deploys more
troops at once:

| Castle level | Armies in the field        | Troops per deploy              |
| ------------ | -------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| 1            | {FRONTIER.realm.armies[0]} | {FRONTIER.realm.deployCaps[0]} |
| 2            | {FRONTIER.realm.armies[1]} | {FRONTIER.realm.deployCaps[1]} |
| 3            | {FRONTIER.realm.armies[2]} | {FRONTIER.realm.deployCaps[2]} |
| 4            | {FRONTIER.realm.armies[3]} | {FRONTIER.realm.deployCaps[3]} |

## Research

At the castle, each building type climbs from common to legendary, paid in Essence and labor. A tier lifts every
building of its type at once, and the choice a tier asks for is final for the season: farms and workshops make{" "}
{FRONTIER.research.outputStep} more or store more, barracks train {FRONTIER.research.outputStep} more or deploy for{" "}
{FRONTIER.research.rationStep} less wheat, and each hut tier houses {FRONTIER.research.populationStep} more.

| Type                   | Uncommon                                                                                     | Rare                                                                                         | Epic                                                                                         | Legendary                                                                                    |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Farm                   | {FRONTIER.research.farm[0].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.farm[0].labor} labor         | {FRONTIER.research.farm[1].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.farm[1].labor} labor         | {FRONTIER.research.farm[2].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.farm[2].labor} labor         | {FRONTIER.research.farm[3].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.farm[3].labor} labor         |
| Workshop               | {FRONTIER.research.workshop[0].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.workshop[0].labor} labor | {FRONTIER.research.workshop[1].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.workshop[1].labor} labor | {FRONTIER.research.workshop[2].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.workshop[2].labor} labor | {FRONTIER.research.workshop[3].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.workshop[3].labor} labor |
| Barracks               | {FRONTIER.research.barracks[0].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.barracks[0].labor} labor | {FRONTIER.research.barracks[1].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.barracks[1].labor} labor | {FRONTIER.research.barracks[2].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.barracks[2].labor} labor | {FRONTIER.research.barracks[3].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.barracks[3].labor} labor |
| Workers' hut           | {FRONTIER.research.hut[0].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.hut[0].labor} labor           | {FRONTIER.research.hut[1].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.hut[1].labor} labor           | {FRONTIER.research.hut[2].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.hut[2].labor} labor           | {FRONTIER.research.hut[3].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.hut[3].labor} labor           |
| Each training building | {FRONTIER.research.training[0].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.training[0].labor} labor | {FRONTIER.research.training[1].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.training[1].labor} labor | {FRONTIER.research.training[2].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.training[2].labor} labor | {FRONTIER.research.training[3].essence} Essence, {FRONTIER.research.training[3].labor} labor |

**Training buildings.** Once your barracks reach {FRONTIER.research.trainingGate}, raise a War hall, a Supply yard, a
Scouts' lodge and a Hearth. Each of their tiers starts every new army a tier higher in Battle, Logistics, Scouting or
Homecoming.

**Shrines and wells.** Research them ({FRONTIER.research.shrine.essence} and {FRONTIER.research.well.essence} Essence)
before reveals can find them.

**The Ethereal depths.** Research opens them one at a time, for {FRONTIER.research.depths[0].essence},{" "}
{FRONTIER.research.depths[1].essence}, then {FRONTIER.research.depths[2].essence} Essence
([The expedition](/frontier/expedition#the-spire-and-its-depths)).

## Labor from your Realms

Each Realm in your payout wallet gives your Frontier realm {FRONTIER.labor.perRealm} labor once a day: open Realms from
your realm's panel, add each by its number, then **Claim all**. Link a payout wallet first
([Payout wallet](/account/payout-wallet)).
