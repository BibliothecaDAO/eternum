# Your realm

Your realm trains troops, grows wheat, and makes labor for your armies. Research at your castle upgrades your buildings
and opens deeper map layers. A new realm starts with {FRONTIER.realm.troops} troops, {FRONTIER.realm.wheat} wheat and
{FRONTIER.realm.labor} labor.

## Buildings

Tap an open plot to build. Your castle makes {FRONTIER.realm.castleLaborRates[0]} labor per hour at Settlement,
multiplying by {FRONTIER.realm.castleLaborStep} at each castle level up to {FRONTIER.realm.castleLaborRates[3]}/h at
Empire. Each building opens at its castle level and costs labor (plus Essence for special buildings):

{FRONTIER.realm.castleLabor[2]} labor. Each level opens more plots, adds one army slot, raises the max troops per

## Research

upgrades every building of that type at once. Farms, Workshops, and Conscription add {FRONTIER.research.outputStep} per
rank; Rations cuts {FRONTIER.realm.rationStep} deploy wheat per rank; Logistics adds {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsStep}

### Oath slots

Every army starts with {FRONTIER.oaths.baseSlots} Oath slots. You can research {FRONTIER.oaths.extraSlots} extra slots
for Knights, Crossbowmen, and Scouts (up to {FRONTIER.oaths.maxSlots} total).

Price per troop kind: {FRONTIER.research.oathSlotsSummary}.

### Castle lore

One sequential ladder, paid in Essence only.

1. **<GameIcon name="well" /> Well lore** ({FRONTIER.research.lore[0].essence} Essence) lets reveals find Wells.
2. **<GameIcon name="shrine" /> Shrine lore** ({FRONTIER.research.lore[1].essence} Essence) lets reveals find Shrines.
3. **<GameIcon name="spire" /> Spire lore** ({FRONTIER.research.lore[2].essence} Essence) lets reveals find Spires to
   enter the Veil, Hollow, and [the Deep](/frontier/deep).

## Daily quests

Complete today's quest, then press **Collect** to receive {FRONTIER.quest.labor} Labor and {FRONTIER.quest.essence}
Essence at castle level 1 (doubling at each higher castle level). A held Realm or the Ancient Seal boosts quest labor to
{FRONTIER.quest.bonusPercent}, and a held Wonder adds {FRONTIER.quest.wonderFragments} Fragments. See
[Quests and banes](/frontier/quests-and-banes) for every quest and bonus.
