# Your Realm

## Founding

Open the Frontier season and found a realm. It keeps its catalogue name, your player name comes from your Realms
profile, and from then on it is raised again on every new map, in its own stretch of wilderness. Entry is free and open
for the whole season, so a player who arrives in week five starts the same way as one who arrived on day one.

A new realm starts with 1,500 troops, 1,000 wheat, 2,000 labor, no Essence and six plots around the castle.

## The board

Upgrades are buildings you place on the realm's own small board. Six buildings, one job each:

| Building     | Produces         | Its job                             | Wants as a neighbour |
| ------------ | ---------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Castle       | Labor            | You can always build. Grants plots. | —                    |
| Farm         | Wheat            | Feeds training and marching         | Storehouse           |
| Barracks     | Troops           | How much you can commit tomorrow    | Farm, Castle         |
| Workshop     | Labor            | Builds and upgrades faster          | Castle               |
| Storehouse   | Storage capacity | Absence costs less                  | Farm, Workshop       |
| Workers' hut | Population       | How many buildings you can have     | Castle               |

Every building but the workers' hut takes population: a farm 1, a workshop or storehouse 2, barracks 3. A realm starts
with room for 6; each workers' hut adds 6, and 2 more beside the castle.

Every bonus is a gain, never a loss. A hex has six sides, you have six plots at first, and every building wants a
neighbour that some other building also wants, so a plot spent on one pair denies another. When you choose a plot, the
build menu lists each building's wanted neighbours and marks the ones that plot already has.

Each further copy of the same building costs more labor than the last. Knocking a building down refunds half its labor,
and a demolition that would leave the realm over capacity is refused. Storage burns the overflow: production never
pauses, and the cap holds about a day of output.

The board is paid in labor, never in Essence.

## The three ladders

Essence found on the map is the only upgrade currency.

| Ladder     | Rung 1  | Rung 2  | Rung 3  |
| ---------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| Castle     | 3,000   | 20,000  | 90,000  |
| Barracks   | 8,000   | 45,000  | —       |
| Attunement | 150,000 | 330,000 | 850,000 |

- **Castle.** Armies in the field at once (3, then 4, 5 and 6), troops per army (3,000 tier I troops, then 9,000, 25,000
  and 60,000), and the plot rings around it (6 plots, then 18, 36 and 60).
- **Barracks.** Which troops the barracks train: tier I, then tier II, then tier III. Troops already trained keep their
  tier.
- **Attunement.** Opens the Ethereal layer below the surface, one depth at a time, through today's spire.

The realm panel shows each ladder's next rung and its Essence price.
