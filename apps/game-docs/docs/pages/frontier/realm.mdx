# Your Realm

## Founding

Your realm is founded the first time you enter the Frontier season, and from then on it is raised again on every new
map, in its own stretch of wilderness. Entry is free and open for the whole season, so a player who arrives late starts
the same way as one who arrived on day one.

A new realm starts with {FRONTIER.realm.troops} troops, {FRONTIER.realm.wheat} wheat and {FRONTIER.realm.labor} labor.

price. Barracks train for free; deploying costs {FRONTIER.realm.deployWheat} wheat a troop.

## Research

building of its type at once, and the choice a tier asks for is final for the season: farms and workshops make{" "}
{FRONTIER.research.outputStep} more or store more, barracks train {FRONTIER.research.outputStep} more or deploy for{" "}
{FRONTIER.research.rationStep} less wheat, and each hut tier houses {FRONTIER.research.populationStep} more.

**Training buildings.** Once your barracks reach {FRONTIER.research.trainingGate}, raise a War hall, a Supply yard, a
Scouts' lodge and a Hearth. Each of their tiers starts every new army a tier higher in Battle, Logistics, Scouting or
Homecoming.

**Shrines and wells.** Research them ({FRONTIER.research.shrine.essence} and {FRONTIER.research.well.essence} Essence)
before reveals can find them.

**The Ethereal depths.** Research opens them one at a time, for {FRONTIER.research.depths[0].essence},{" "}
{FRONTIER.research.depths[1].essence}, then {FRONTIER.research.depths[2].essence} Essence.

## Armies learn

An army earns XP from reveals, clears and shrines, and spends it on the attribute tiers you choose, up to legendary:
{FRONTIER.xp.tierPrices[0]}, {FRONTIER.xp.tierPrices[1]}, {FRONTIER.xp.tierPrices[2]}, then{" "}
{FRONTIER.xp.tierPrices[3]} XP for each next tier. Battle hits up to {FRONTIER.attributes.battleMax} harder, Logistics
carries up to{" "} {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsMax} more stamina, and each Scouting tier adds
{FRONTIER.attributes.scouting[0]},{" "} {FRONTIER.attributes.scouting[1]}, {FRONTIER.attributes.scouting[2]}, then
{FRONTIER.attributes.scouting[3]} to the find rate of the camps, rifts or beasts you pick for it, so all four on one
kind add{" "} {FRONTIER.attributes.scoutingOneKind}. Homecoming sends survivors home when the day ends
([The Expedition](/frontier/expedition)).
