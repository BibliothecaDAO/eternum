# Your realm

Your realm makes the troops, wheat and labor your armies need, and grows stronger with research. A new realm starts with
{FRONTIER.realm.troops} troops, {FRONTIER.realm.wheat} wheat and {FRONTIER.realm.labor} labor.

## Research

every building of its type at once. Each Farm and Workshop tier adds {FRONTIER.research.outputStep} more output. Each
Hut tier houses {FRONTIER.research.populationStep} more workers.

**Storerooms** open at {FRONTIER.storeroom.opens}, one per realm. A Storeroom adds {FRONTIER.storeroom.storageStep} to
the wheat and labor your castle stores.

**Supply yards** open at {FRONTIER.supplyYardOpens}, one per realm. Construction includes Logistics rank I for new
armies. Research raises that starting rank to II–V. Rebuilding grants no extra rank.

**Scouts’ lodges** open at City and can be built more than once. Each produces its own Scouts.

**The castle's lore.** One ladder, in this order: Well lore ({FRONTIER.research.lore[0].essence} Essence) lets reveals
find wells, Shrine lore ({FRONTIER.research.lore[1].essence}) shrines, and Spire lore
({FRONTIER.research.lore[2].essence}) spires, which open every Ethereal depth
([The expedition](/frontier/expedition#the-spire-and-its-depths)).

## Daily quests

Complete today's quest, then use **Collect** to receive {FRONTIER.quest.labor} Labor and {FRONTIER.quest.essence}
Essence. Quest progress and collection belong to your home. Daily quests replace the old Realm Labor claim in the
current season; a held Realm does not add a second daily grant.
