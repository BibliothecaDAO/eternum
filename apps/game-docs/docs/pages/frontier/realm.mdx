# Your realm

Your realm trains troops, grows wheat, and makes labor for your armies. Research at your castle upgrades every building
of a kind at once, unlocks extra Oath slots, and opens deeper map layers.

A new realm starts with {FRONTIER.realm.troops} {FRONTIER.troops.knight} waiting at home (no army is deployed yet),
{FRONTIER.realm.wheat} wheat, and {FRONTIER.realm.labor} labor. That starting labor buys
{FRONTIER.realm.firstMinuteSummary}. You cannot trade or send resources to another player.

There is no population cap in Frontier. Each plot holds one building, and demolishing a building refunds
{FRONTIER.realm.demolitionRefund} of the labor you paid for it. Training buildings produce troops for free; deploying
troops into an army costs {FRONTIER.realm.deployWheat} wheat per troop before Rations discounts.

### Unique buildings (one per realm)

- **<GameIcon name={FRONTIER.buildingIcons.storeroom} /> {FRONTIER.names.storeroom}** (opens at
  {FRONTIER.storeroom.opens}) adds {FRONTIER.storeroom.storageStep} to your wheat and labor storage caps (doubling them)
  and scales with your castle level.
- **<GameIcon name={FRONTIER.buildingIcons.conscriptionCamp} /> {FRONTIER.names.conscriptionCamp}** (opens at
  {FRONTIER.conscriptionOpens}) grants Conscription rank I, speeding up troop training by {FRONTIER.research.outputStep}
  per rank across every {FRONTIER.names.barracks}, {FRONTIER.names.range}, and {FRONTIER.names.scoutsLodge}.
- **<GameIcon name={FRONTIER.buildingIcons.rationsDepot} /> {FRONTIER.names.rationsDepot}** (opens at
  {FRONTIER.rationsOpens}) grants Rations rank I, cutting deploy wheat cost by {FRONTIER.realm.rationStep} wheat per
  troop per rank.
- **<GameIcon name={FRONTIER.buildingIcons.supplyYard} /> {FRONTIER.names.supplyYard}** (opens at
  {FRONTIER.supplyYardOpens}) grants new armies Logistics rank I, raising max stamina by
  {FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsStep} per rank. Rebuilding a unique building does not grant a second rank.

{FRONTIER.realm.castleLabor[2]} labor. Each level's labor store holds the full price of the next upgrade. Raising your
castle opens another ring of plots, adds an army slot, raises the max troops per deploy, doubles castle labor, and

### Oath slots

Every army starts with {FRONTIER.oaths.baseSlots} Oath slots. You can research {FRONTIER.oaths.extraSlots} extra slots
separately for {FRONTIER.troops.knight} (at your {FRONTIER.names.barracks}), {FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman} (at your
{FRONTIER.names.range}), and {FRONTIER.troops.scout} (at your {FRONTIER.names.scoutsLodge}), up to
{FRONTIER.oaths.maxSlots} total. Unlocked Oath slots stay for the whole season and apply to every new army of that troop
kind ([Oaths and relics](/frontier/oaths-and-relics)).

Price per troop kind: {FRONTIER.research.oathSlotsSummary}.

### Castle lore

Research these three in order. They cost only Essence.

1. **<GameIcon name="well" /> Well lore** ({FRONTIER.research.lore[0].essence} Essence) lets reveals find Wells.
2. **<GameIcon name="shrine" /> Shrine lore** ({FRONTIER.research.lore[1].essence} Essence) lets reveals find Shrines.
3. **<GameIcon name="spire" /> Spire lore** ({FRONTIER.research.lore[2].essence} Essence) lets reveals find Spires to
   enter the {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, {FRONTIER.depths[2].name}, and [the {FRONTIER.names.deep}](/frontier/deep).

## Daily quests

Complete today's quest, then press **Collect** to receive {FRONTIER.quest.labor} Labor and {FRONTIER.quest.essence}
Essence at {FRONTIER.realm.castleLevelInline[0]}, multiplying by {FRONTIER.quest.levelStep} at each higher castle level.
A **Realm NFT** or the Ancient Seal boosts quest labor to {FRONTIER.quest.bonusPercent}, and a **Wonder Realm NFT** adds
+{FRONTIER.quest.wonderFragments} Fragments. See [Quests and banes](/frontier/quests-and-banes) for every quest and
bonus.
