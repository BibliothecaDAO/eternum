# The Deep

The Deep is the shared fourth layer where all {FRONTIER.aspectsCount} Aspects meet. Every player in the season shares
one {FRONTIER.deep.mapSize} by {FRONTIER.deep.mapSize} Deep map. There are no tiles to reveal in the Deep: every site is
visible from the start of the day. Armies moving in the open cannot be attacked and do not block each other; player
fights happen at the sites.

## Entering the Deep

1. Research **Spire lore** at your castle ([Realm and research](/frontier/realm#castle-lore)).
2. Descend through your {FRONTIER.depths[0].name}, {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, and {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} Spires (0
   stamina each).
3. Your army arrives at your Aspect's gate, {FRONTIER.deep.gateDistance} tiles from the center: {FRONTIER.deep.gates}.
   If the gate tile is occupied, the army lands on the nearest free tile so gates cannot be blocked.
4. Once inside, the army stays in the Deep for the rest of the day, and Fragment stamina refills and Oath rerolls do not
   work inside the Deep.

## Rings and realms in the Deep

The Deep has {FRONTIER.deep.ringsCount} concentric rings around the center, with {FRONTIER.deep.sitesPerRing} sites on
each ring ({FRONTIER.deep.sitesTotal} sites total): one of each of the {FRONTIER.deep.sitesCount} site kinds plus
{FRONTIER.deep.randomSitesPerRing} random sites, so Ring 1 alone can hold 1 to {FRONTIER.deep.maxSameKindPerRing}
Hyperstructure archives. Ring 1 is open from the start of each day. Outer rings open for the day once enough realms have
sent an army into the Deep that day:

| Ring                                 | Distance from center                  | Realms sent into the Deep today               |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Ring {FRONTIER.deep.rings[0].number} | {FRONTIER.deep.rings[0].radius} tiles | {FRONTIER.deep.rings[0].realms} (always open) |
| Ring {FRONTIER.deep.rings[1].number} | {FRONTIER.deep.rings[1].radius} tiles | {FRONTIER.deep.rings[1].realms}               |
| Ring {FRONTIER.deep.rings[2].number} | {FRONTIER.deep.rings[2].radius} tiles | {FRONTIER.deep.rings[2].realms}               |
| Ring {FRONTIER.deep.rings[3].number} | {FRONTIER.deep.rings[3].radius} tiles | {FRONTIER.deep.rings[3].realms}               |

## The {FRONTIER.deep.sitesCount} site kinds and their beasts

Every game day, fresh beasts guard every open Deep site. Deep beasts take no tick drain and do not respawn once cleared
that day. Select any open site to see its beast portrait and blade or bow badge, its per-tick payout rates, and its
green poison drain icon. Locked sites show only the reward kinds they pay until their ring opens. Clearing the last
defender claims the site for your player. Your army stays beside the site, and every guard slot starts empty. Sites with
garrisoned troops pay out on every army tick ({FRONTIER.stamina.tickMinutes} minutes):

**Glory** is earned **only** by holding Hyperstructure archives and Bane altars. Essence cores and Ethereal mines pay
your realm directly in Essence or labor and give no Glory. Every Deep site's tick payout — including an Essence core's
{FRONTIER.deep.corePerTick} Essence and an Ethereal mine's {FRONTIER.deep.minePerTick} labor — is one pool for the whole
site, split among its surviving guards by troop count.

## Guard slots, troop drain, and PvP

- **Guard slots:** a claimed Deep site has {FRONTIER.deep.guardSlots} guard slots. Transfer troops from an adjacent army
  to garrison it. The owner's Aspect can garrison {FRONTIER.deep.alliedSites}. Only the owner can garrison
  {FRONTIER.deep.ownerSites}.
- **Slot capacity:** each slot holds one player's troops of a single type and tier, up to that player's army cap. A
  player can use multiple slots with different troop types or tiers. Only the player who supplied a slot can withdraw
  its troops, into their own adjacent army.
- **Tick order (drain before pay):** every tick ({FRONTIER.stamina.tickMinutes} minutes), every army in the Deep gains
  +{FRONTIER.stamina.perTick} stamina and loses {FRONTIER.deep.lossPerTick} troops (reduced to
  {FRONTIER.oaths.vitriolLoss} per tick with the Vitriol Oath). Garrison slots lose {FRONTIER.deep.lossPerTick} troops
  per tick, and an empty slot is free to fill again. Held sites then pay the guards that survived the tick, split by
  their troop count.
- **Strike or wait:** because rival guards lose {FRONTIER.deep.lossPerTick} troops every tick, waiting lets you attack a
  weaker guard later while the holder earns ticks in the meantime.
- **Attack order:** rival attackers fight one guard at a time, starting with the highest occupied slot. Guards keep
  their losses, so allies attacking in turn can weaken a site together. The player who clears the last guard captures
  the site. Allies can never attack allies.
- **Guard protection:** garrisoned troops have no Oaths. Knights in a guard slot take
  {FRONTIER.attributes.knightGuardReduction} less damage.
- **Daily reset:** sites return to their beasts at the start of each game day. Any troops left in the garrison are lost.
- **Shared building pay:** each open Hyperstructure archive pays {FRONTIER.deep.payAlone} per tick if 1 Archive is open,
  {FRONTIER.deep.payPair} if 2 are open, or {FRONTIER.deep.payCrowd} if 3 or more are open — counting **open Archives**
  across the Deep, not rings unlocked. That Glory goes **only to the guards holding that Archive** (split by troop
  count), while the **whole Aspect** gains the same amount of hyperstructure height. If a rival Team Bane pauses your
  Aspect's hyperstructure height, Archive guards still earn their full Glory each tick
  ([Banes and Triumphs](/frontier/banes#rival-team-banes)). Open Bane altars pay the same {FRONTIER.deep.payAlone},
  {FRONTIER.deep.payPair}, or {FRONTIER.deep.payCrowd} bane bar charge and Glory per tick based on how many altars are
  open, with Glory split among the altar's guards.
- **Bane altar trigger:** when your Aspect's bane bar reaches {FRONTIER.deep.altarThreshold}, it resets to 0 and sends a
  Team Bane onto the rival Aspect with the tallest hyperstructure for the next game day, skipping any rival already
  blocked or already targeted for tomorrow. No Team Bane is issued on the last day of the season
  ([Banes and Triumphs](/frontier/banes#rival-team-banes)).
