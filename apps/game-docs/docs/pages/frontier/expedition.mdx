# The expedition

## Today's map

Every game day starts with a new hidden map. Your realm appears with its six neighboring tiles already explored. Each
reveal opens exactly 1 tile — the tile your army moves into; no relic or Oath reveals multiple tiles at once. Walking
over explored tiles costs {FRONTIER.stamina.step} stamina per step and finds nothing.

## What a reveal pays

Every tile an army reveals sends Essence or labor straight to your realm (50% chance of each). The payout is a share of
the army's troop count, capped at {FRONTIER.discovery.revealTroopCap} troops counted per reveal, and grows on deeper
layers:

| Layer                     | Reveal pays, of the army's troops  |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| {FRONTIER.depths[0].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].revealPercent} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[1].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].revealPercent} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].revealPercent} |

and guard size by layer):

| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <GameIcon name={FRONTIER.beastFamilies[0].icon} /> Herd | {FRONTIER.discovery.herd}                                                                  | Blade guard. Clearing it pays wheat ({FRONTIER.sitePayouts.herdMultiplier} its starting guard on the {FRONTIER.depths[0].name}, {FRONTIER.sitePayouts.veilMultiplier} of that in the {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, {FRONTIER.sitePayouts.hollowMultiplier} in the {FRONTIER.depths[2].name}).                           |

After {FRONTIER.discovery.emptyRevealLimit} empty reveals in a row (or {FRONTIER.oaths.detectionLimit} with the
Detection Oath), the next reveal is guaranteed to find a discovery.

## Fragment caches in ruins

<GameIcon name="fragment" /> Each ruin holds **either** a relic **or** a Fragment cache, never both. You see whether a
ruin holds a Fragment cache before you attack it. Cache odds are {FRONTIER.caches.surface} on the
{FRONTIER.depths[0].name}, {FRONTIER.caches.veil} in the {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, and {FRONTIER.caches.hollow} in the
{FRONTIER.depths[2].name}. A cache pays 1 Fragment per {FRONTIER.caches.troopsPerFragment} guard troops of that ruin to
the account whose army lands the last hit.

## Supply posts

Select any camp you cleared today to open its **Supply Post** panel, which shows your home troop stock, when the post
expires, and every adjacent army in range. Click an army in the panel (or right-click a lit army on the map on desktop,
or hold it on a phone) to add troops to that army from home, paying the same wheat cost as deploying at home. Supply
posts work on the {FRONTIER.depths[0].name}, {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, and {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} until the day
ends. Clearing a camp in the {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} lets you top up an army right before it enters
[the Deep](/frontier/deep).

## The Spire and deeper layers

Spire lore ([Realm and research](/frontier/realm#castle-lore)) lets you find one Spire per personal layer each day,
starting at {FRONTIER.discovery.spireStep} on your first reveal of that layer and rising by
{FRONTIER.discovery.spireStep} each reveal until guaranteed on reveal {FRONTIER.discovery.spireRevealLimit}.

Each Spire leads one layer down ({FRONTIER.depths[0].name} to {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, {FRONTIER.depths[1].name} to
{FRONTIER.depths[2].name}, and {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} to [the {FRONTIER.names.deep}](/frontier/deep)). Move an army
beside your Spire and tap **Enter**. Descending costs 0 stamina, and the army stays on that layer for the rest of the
day. Send {FRONTIER.troops.scout} ahead to find each Spire cheaply, then move your fighting armies through it.
