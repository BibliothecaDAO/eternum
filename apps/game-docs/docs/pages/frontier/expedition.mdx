# The expedition

## Today's map

Every game day starts with a new hidden map. Your realm appears with its six neighboring tiles already explored. Walking
over explored tiles costs {FRONTIER.stamina.step} stamina per step and finds nothing.

## What a reveal pays

Every tile an army reveals sends Essence or labor straight to your realm (50% chance of each). The payout is a share of
the army's troop count, capped at {FRONTIER.discovery.revealTroopCap} troops counted per reveal, and grows on deeper
layers:

| Layer                     | Reveal pays, of the army's troops  |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| {FRONTIER.depths[0].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].revealPercent} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[1].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].revealPercent} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].revealPercent} |

## What a reveal can find

| Find                              | Chance                      | What it gives                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <GameIcon name="beasts" /> Beasts | {FRONTIER.discovery.beasts} | Either a **herd** (clearing it pays wheat) or a larger **band** (clearing it triggers an Oath draw and pays no resources).                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| <GameIcon name="camp" /> Camp     | {FRONTIER.discovery.camp}   | Guarded camp. Clearing it pays labor ({FRONTIER.sitePayouts.campPercent} of its starting guard on the {FRONTIER.depths[0].name}, {FRONTIER.sitePayouts.veilMultiplier} of that in the {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, {FRONTIER.sitePayouts.hollowMultiplier} in the {FRONTIER.depths[2].name}) and creates a supply post. |
| <GameIcon name="rift" /> Rift     | {FRONTIER.discovery.rift}   | Guarded fissure. Clearing it pays Essence ({FRONTIER.sitePayouts.riftPercent} of its starting guard on the {FRONTIER.depths[0].name}, {FRONTIER.sitePayouts.veilMultiplier} of that in the {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, {FRONTIER.sitePayouts.hollowMultiplier} in the {FRONTIER.depths[2].name}).                      |
| <GameIcon name="ruin" /> Ruin     | {FRONTIER.discovery.ruin}   | Fallen realm. Clearing it awards its relic and can also hold a Fragment cache.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| <GameIcon name="shrine" /> Shrine | {FRONTIER.discovery.shrine} | Needs Shrine lore. Draws {FRONTIER.oaths.drawCount} Oaths to pick from, or lets you pass for +{FRONTIER.oaths.passStamina} stamina.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| <GameIcon name="well" /> Well     | {FRONTIER.discovery.well}   | Needs Well lore. Refills {FRONTIER.stamina.well} once.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

After {FRONTIER.discovery.emptyRevealLimit} empty reveals in a row (or {FRONTIER.oaths.detectionLimit} with the
Detection Oath), the next reveal is guaranteed to find a discovery. Once you research Spire lore, your Spire is
guaranteed to appear within {FRONTIER.discovery.spireRevealLimit} reveals.

## Guards

You see every guard before you attack. Camp, rift, herd, and band guards double in strength on each deeper layer. Ruin
guards follow the ruin's relic tier instead.

| Layer                     | Camps                          | Rifts                          | Herds                          | Bands                            | Ruins                          |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| {FRONTIER.depths[0].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].campGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].riftGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].beastWeak} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].beastStrong} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].ruinGuard} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[1].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].campGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].riftGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].beastWeak} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].beastStrong} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].ruinGuard} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].campGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].riftGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].beastWeak} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].beastStrong} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].ruinGuard} |

Bands appear {FRONTIER.discovery.bandsPerHerd} times as often as herds. Ruin guard sizes by layer and relic tier:

| Layer                              | Lowest tier                      | Middle tier                      | Highest tier                      |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |

## Fragment caches in ruins

<GameIcon name="fragment" /> Clearing a ruin can also award free Fragments from a hidden cache:
{FRONTIER.caches.surface} chance on the {FRONTIER.depths[0].name}, {FRONTIER.caches.veil} in the
{FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, and {FRONTIER.caches.hollow} in the {FRONTIER.depths[2].name}. A cache pays 1 Fragment per
{FRONTIER.caches.troopsPerFragment} guard troops of that ruin.

## Supply posts

Select any camp you cleared today and pick an adjacent army to add troops to that army from your home troop stock,
paying the same wheat cost as deploying at home. Supply posts work on the {FRONTIER.depths[0].name},
{FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, and {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} until the day ends.

## The Spire and deeper layers

Spire lore ([Your realm](/frontier/realm#castle-lore)) lets you enter the {FRONTIER.depths[1].name},
{FRONTIER.depths[2].name}, and the shared [{FRONTIER.names.deep}](/frontier/deep). Select an army beside your Spire and
tap **Enter**. Descending costs no stamina, and the army stays on that layer for the rest of the day.
