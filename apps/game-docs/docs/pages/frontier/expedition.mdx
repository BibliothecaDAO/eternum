# The Expedition

## Today's map

Every expedition starts on fresh fog. Your armies start the day on your home ring, the seven tiles at the heart of your
own region, and they can move only inside that region. The ring counts as explored from the first second, so crossing it
costs travel stamina and finds nothing. Nobody arrives too late and no map is farmed dry.

## What a tile can hold

| Tile        | Where       | Chance    | What it gives                                                                  |
| ----------- | ----------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Supplies    | Every layer | New tiles | 50–300 Essence, 60–450 labor or 30–250 troops, sent straight home.             |
| Rift        | Surface     | 4%        | Guarded. Captured, it produces Essence into the realm until the day ends.      |
| Camp        | Every layer | About 6%  | A guarded outpost. Clearing it pays its chest and nothing else.                |
| Trove       | Ethereal    | 4%        | Rifts give way to troves below: heavily guarded, producing more, with a chest. |
| Loose chest | Every layer | About 1%  | A lucky find on an empty tile, on top of the chests you fight for.             |

Supplies come with every tile an army reveals, including the ones where a camp or rift appears, and deeper ground
multiplies them. Walking over known ground pays nothing. Everything found goes home the moment it is picked up, and an
army never carries anything worth losing.

## Merging armies

An army can grow in the field only by absorbing another of your armies from the same realm. They must stand side by side
and have the same troop type and tier. The merged army keeps the lower of the two staminas, so a merge never refills a
tired army, and it can hold no more troops than your castle allows per army.

## Fighting a camp

A camp shows its exact guard before you attack. Guards run from 1,000 to 1,599 on the surface, 3,000 to 4,999 at
Ethereal I, 8,000 to 11,999 at Ethereal II and 20,000 to 29,999 at Ethereal III, so what counts as a safe fight moves as
the realm grows. Winning opens the site's chest where the army stands. No fight refunds stamina, won or lost.

## The spire and its depths

Today's spire stands on one tile of your home ring and moves one step around the ring each day. Once your realm is
attuned, bring an army onto or beside the spire, tap it, and pick a depth you have unlocked. Entering costs 30 stamina
for Ethereal I, 40 for II and 50 for III, and the trip is one way for the day. Supplies below are four times the surface
at Ethereal I and double again each step down, with guards to match.

## The rollover

At 00:00 UTC the expedition ends. The map and its fog, every camp, rift and trove, and every army you sent out are gone.
Your realm is raised on the next map with its castle, barracks, plots, spire, uncommitted garrison and stored wheat,
labor and Essence. The game tells you when the new expedition has begun and takes you to your realm.
