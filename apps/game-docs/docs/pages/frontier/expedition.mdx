# The expedition and beasts

## Today's map

Every game day starts with a new hidden map. Your realm appears with its six neighboring tiles already explored. Walking
over explored tiles costs {FRONTIER.stamina.step} stamina per step and finds nothing.

## What a reveal pays

Every tile an army reveals sends Essence or labor straight to your realm (50% chance of each). The payout is a share of
the army's troop count, capped at {FRONTIER.discovery.revealTroopCap} troops counted per reveal, and grows on deeper
layers:

| Layer                     | Reveal pays, of the army's troops  |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| {FRONTIER.depths[0].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].revealPercent} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[1].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].revealPercent} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].revealPercent} |

and reward stay the same. Camp, rift, herd, and band guards double in size on each deeper layer (each guard varies by
10%). Ruin guards follow the ruin's relic tier instead.

## Fragment caches in ruins

<GameIcon name="fragment" /> Each ruin holds **either** a relic **or** a Fragment cache, never both. You see whether a
ruin holds a Fragment cache before you attack it. Cache odds are {FRONTIER.caches.surface} on the
{FRONTIER.depths[0].name}, {FRONTIER.caches.veil} in the {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, and {FRONTIER.caches.hollow} in the
{FRONTIER.depths[2].name}. A cache pays 1 Fragment per {FRONTIER.caches.troopsPerFragment} guard troops of that ruin to
the account whose army lands the last hit.

## Supply posts

Select any camp you cleared today and pick an adjacent army to add troops to that army from your home troop stock,
paying the same wheat cost as deploying at home. Supply posts work on the {FRONTIER.depths[0].name},
{FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, and {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} until the day ends. Clearing a camp in the
{FRONTIER.depths[2].name} lets you top up an army right before it enters [the Deep](/frontier/deep).

## The Spire and deeper layers

Spire lore ([Your realm](/frontier/realm#castle-lore)) lets you find one Spire per personal layer each day, starting at
{FRONTIER.discovery.spireStep} on your first reveal of that layer and rising by {FRONTIER.discovery.spireStep} each
reveal until guaranteed on reveal {FRONTIER.discovery.spireRevealLimit}.

Each Spire leads one layer down ({FRONTIER.depths[0].name} to {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, {FRONTIER.depths[1].name} to
{FRONTIER.depths[2].name}, and {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} to [the {FRONTIER.names.deep}](/frontier/deep)). Move an army
beside your Spire and tap **Enter**. Descending costs 0 stamina, and the army stays on that layer for the rest of the
day. Send {FRONTIER.troops.scout} ahead to find each Spire cheaply, then march your fighting armies through it.
