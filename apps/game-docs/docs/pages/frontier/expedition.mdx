# The Expedition

## Today's map

Every expedition starts on fresh fog. Your realm stands at the edge of its own region, and armies can move only inside
that region. Nobody arrives too late and no map is farmed dry.

## What a tile can hold

| Tile        | Where       | Chance     | What it gives                                                   |
| ----------- | ----------- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Supplies    | Every layer | Most tiles | Essence, labor or troops, sent straight home. No tile is empty. |
| Rift        | Surface     | Rare       | Guarded Essence, most of a day's haul; produces into the realm. |
| Camp        | Every layer | Rare       | A guarded outpost. Clear it for its chest and 25 stamina back.  |
| Trove       | Ethereal    | Rare       | Rifts give way to troves below: heavily guarded, with a chest.  |
| Loose chest | Every layer | Rare       | A lucky find in the fog, on top of the ones you fight for.      |

Everything found on the map goes home the moment it is picked up. An army never grows in the field and never carries
anything worth losing. Troops found today join tomorrow's muster.

## Fighting a camp

A camp shows its strength before you attack. Guards run 800 to 1,600 on the surface and far more below, so what counts
as a safe fight moves as the realm grows. Winning refunds 25 stamina and opens the site's chest where the army stands.
Losing refunds nothing.

## The spire and its depths

Once your realm is attuned, a spire stands on its ring. Bring an army beside your realm, tap the spire, and pick a depth
you have unlocked. Entering costs stamina, more for deeper ground, and the trip is one way for the day. Deeper ground
pays roughly four times the surface at Ethereal I, and double again each step down, with guards to match.

## The rollover

At 00:00 UTC the expedition ends. The map and its fog, every camp, rift and trove, and every army you sent out are gone.
Your realm is raised on the next map with its castle, barracks, plots, spire, uncommitted garrison and stored wheat,
labor and Essence. The game tells you when the new expedition has begun and takes you to your realm.
