# The expedition

## Today's map

Every day starts on fresh fog. Your realm is raised in its own stretch of the new map, and its six neighbouring tiles
start the day explored. Walking over known ground costs {FRONTIER.stamina.step} stamina a step and finds nothing.

<Video name="frontier/explore" caption="An army walks into the fog, and each tile it reveals is drawn as it lands." />

## What a reveal pays

Every tile an army reveals sends Essence or labor home to the realm, as likely as each other: a share of the army's
troops, larger the deeper it is. Each reveal also gives the army {FRONTIER.xp.reveal.join(" / ")} XP at
{FRONTIER.depths.map((depth) => depth.name).join(" / ")}.

| Ground                    | Reveal pays, of the army's troops  | Entering costs                            |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| {FRONTIER.depths[0].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].revealPercent} | —                                         |
| {FRONTIER.depths[1].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].revealPercent} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].entryStamina} stamina |
| {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].revealPercent} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].entryStamina} stamina |
| {FRONTIER.depths[3].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[3].revealPercent} | {FRONTIER.depths[3].entryStamina} stamina |

## What a reveal can find

| Find   | Chance                                      | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Beasts | {FRONTIER.discovery.beasts}                 | A roaming band, the weakest guard. Clearing it pays XP and labor: one quarter of its starting troops.                                                                                             |
| Camp   | {FRONTIER.discovery.camp}                   | A camp guarded by a {FRONTIER.guards.camp}. Clearing it pays labor: half its guard's starting troops.                                                                                             |
| Rift   | {FRONTIER.discovery.rift}                   | A fissure guarded by a {FRONTIER.guards.rift}. Clearing it pays Essence: three times its guard's starting troops.                                                                                 |
| Ruin   | {FRONTIER.discovery.ruin}                   | A fallen realm, the strongest guard. Clearing it pays its chest in LORDS. Find any number; clear one per realm per day. The other ruins disappear across all depths ([Chests](/frontier/chests)). |
| Shrine | {FRONTIER.discovery.shrine}                 | Once researched: an army beside it uses it for {FRONTIER.xp.shrine} XP, then it is gone.                                                                                                          |
| Well   | {FRONTIER.discovery.well}                   | Once researched: an army beside it uses it for {FRONTIER.stamina.well} stamina, then it is gone.                                                                                                  |
| Chest  | 1 in {FRONTIER.discovery.exploreChestOneIn} | On the surface only, while the day's budget can fund it ([Chests](/frontier/chests)).                                                                                                             |

After {FRONTIER.discovery.emptyRevealLimit} empty reveals in a row, the next reveal draws only among the sites, shrines
and wells above, so it finds one. After a ruin clear, no further ruins appear that day. Scouting makes the kinds you
pick for it more likely ([Armies](/frontier/armies#xp-and-attributes)).

To use a shrine or a well, select an army beside it and right-click it (on a phone, press and hold), or tap it and press
**Use**.

## Guards

Every guard is shown before you attack. Guards grow with depth, so what counts as a safe fight moves as your realm
grows.

| Ground                    | Camps and rifts                | Ruins                          | Weaker beasts                  | Stronger beasts                  |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| {FRONTIER.depths[0].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].siteGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].ruinGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].beastWeak} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].beastStrong} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[1].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].siteGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].ruinGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].beastWeak} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].beastStrong} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].siteGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].ruinGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].beastWeak} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].beastStrong} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[3].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[3].siteGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[3].ruinGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[3].beastWeak} | {FRONTIER.depths[3].beastStrong} |

A band of beasts is a {FRONTIER.guards.weakBeast} or a stronger {FRONTIER.guards.strongBeast}, as likely as each other.
A camp is always guarded by a {FRONTIER.guards.camp} and a rift by a {FRONTIER.guards.rift}. A ruin's guard grows with
depth: {FRONTIER.depths[0].ruinGuardName} on the surface, then {FRONTIER.depths[1].ruinGuardName},{" "}
{FRONTIER.depths[2].ruinGuardName} and {FRONTIER.depths[3].ruinGuardName}. A clear gives the army XP by the guard's
starting strength, beasts included.

## The spire and its depths

Research opens the Ethereal depths, one at a time ([Your realm](/frontier/realm#research)). Your spire stands on one of
the six tiles around your realm, a different one each day. Select an army beside it and tap the spire: it lists each
depth you have researched with its entry stamina. **Enter** takes the army down, and it stays in that depth for the rest
of the day.

<Shot name="frontier/spire" caption="The spire's card: each researched depth and the stamina to enter it." />

## The day's end

When the day ends, the map and its fog, every site and every army you sent out are gone. An army with Homecoming first
sends a share of its survivors home: {FRONTIER.homecoming[0]} at uncommon, {FRONTIER.homecoming[1]} at rare,
{FRONTIER.homecoming[2]} at epic and {FRONTIER.homecoming[3]} at legendary. Your realm is raised on the next map with
its buildings, research, troops and stores, and the game takes you to it.
