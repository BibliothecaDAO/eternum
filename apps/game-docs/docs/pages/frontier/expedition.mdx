# The expedition

## Today's map

Every day starts on fresh fog. Your realm is raised in its own stretch of the new map, and its six neighbouring tiles
start the day explored. Walking over known ground costs {FRONTIER.stamina.step} stamina a step and finds nothing.

<Video name="frontier/explore" caption="An army walks into the fog, and each tile it reveals is drawn as it lands." />

## What a reveal pays

Every tile an army reveals sends Essence or labor home to the realm, as likely as each other: a share of the army's
troops, larger the deeper it is.

| Ground                    | Reveal pays, of the army's troops  |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| {FRONTIER.depths[0].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].revealPercent} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[1].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].revealPercent} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].revealPercent} |

## What a reveal can find

| Find   | Chance                      | What it is                                                                                             |
| ------ | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Beasts | {FRONTIER.discovery.beasts} | Roaming beasts: a weaker herd pays wheat; a stronger band has no resource reward.                      |
| Camp   | {FRONTIER.discovery.camp}   | A guarded camp. Clearing it pays labor and makes it your Realm's supply post for the day.              |
| Rift   | {FRONTIER.discovery.rift}   | A guarded fissure. Clearing it pays Essence: three times its guard's starting troops.                  |
| Ruin   | {FRONTIER.discovery.ruin}   | A fallen realm. Clearing it awards its recorded relic; its rarity sets the guard strength shown below. |

After {FRONTIER.discovery.emptyRevealLimit} empty reveals in a row, the next reveal draws a discovery.

## Guards

Every guard is shown before you attack. Guards grow with depth, so what counts as a safe fight moves as your realm
grows.

| Ground                    | Camps                          | Rifts                          | Weaker beasts                  | Stronger beasts                  | Ruins                          |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| {FRONTIER.depths[0].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].campGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].riftGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].beastWeak} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].beastStrong} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].ruinGuard} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[1].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].campGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].riftGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].beastWeak} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].beastStrong} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].ruinGuard} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].campGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].riftGuard} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].beastWeak} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].beastStrong} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].ruinGuard} |

Roaming beasts are a weaker herd or a stronger band, and a band comes {FRONTIER.discovery.bandsPerHerd} times as often
as a herd. Each family's beast changes with the layer, as the table names it. A ruin's guard size follows its relic's
rarity on every layer. Resource rewards have a depth premium; the site's card shows the full payout before combat.

## The spire and its depths

Spire lore, the last step of the castle's lore ([Your realm](/frontier/realm#research)), opens every Ethereal depth.
Your spire stands on one of the six tiles around your realm, a different one each day. Select an army beside it and tap
the spire: it lists each depth. **Enter** costs no stamina and takes the army down, and it stays in that depth for the
rest of the day.

<Shot name="frontier/spire" caption="The spire's card: each depth and its Enter button." />

## The day's end

When the day ends, the map and its fog, every site and every army you sent out are gone. Your realm is raised on the
next map with its buildings, research, troops and stores, and the game takes you to it.

## Supply posts

Select a camp your Realm cleared today, then choose an adjacent army to reinforce it. Reinforcement uses the same troop
stock and wheat cost as at home. Camps work on the Surface and the two layers above the Deep; their supply service ends
with the day’s map.
