# The expedition

## Today's map

Every game day starts on fresh fog. Your realm rises in its own stretch of wilderness, with its six neighboring tiles
already explored. Walking over explored tiles costs {FRONTIER.stamina.step} stamina per step and finds nothing.

<Video name="frontier/explore" caption="An army walks into the fog, and each tile it reveals is drawn as it lands." />

## What a reveal pays

Every tile an army reveals sends Essence or labor straight to your realm (50% chance of each). The payout is a share of
the army's troop count, capped at {FRONTIER.discovery.revealTroopCap} troops counted per reveal, and grows on deeper
layers:

| Ground                    | Reveal pays, of the army's troops  |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| {FRONTIER.depths[0].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[0].revealPercent} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[1].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[1].revealPercent} |
| {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} | {FRONTIER.depths[2].revealPercent} |

## What a reveal can find

| Find                              | Chance                      | What it gives                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <GameIcon name="beasts" /> Beasts | {FRONTIER.discovery.beasts} | Roaming beasts. Clearing a weaker herd pays wheat; clearing a stronger band triggers an Oath draw (no resources).                 |
| <GameIcon name="camp" /> Camp     | {FRONTIER.discovery.camp}   | Guarded camp. Clearing it pays labor (half its starting guard on the Surface, rising on deeper layers) and creates a supply post. |
| <GameIcon name="rift" /> Rift     | {FRONTIER.discovery.rift}   | Guarded fissure. Clearing it pays Essence (three times its starting guard on the Surface, rising on deeper layers).               |
| <GameIcon name="ruin" /> Ruin     | {FRONTIER.discovery.ruin}   | Fallen realm. Clearing it awards its relic and can also hold a Fragment cache.                                                    |
| <GameIcon name="shrine" /> Shrine | {FRONTIER.discovery.shrine} | Needs Shrine lore. Draws 3 Oaths to pick from, or lets you pass for stamina.                                                      |
| <GameIcon name="well" /> Well     | {FRONTIER.discovery.well}   | Needs Well lore. Refills {FRONTIER.stamina.well} once.                                                                            |

After {FRONTIER.discovery.emptyRevealLimit} empty reveals in a row (or {FRONTIER.oaths.detectionLimit} with the
Detection Oath), the next reveal is guaranteed to find a discovery. Once you research Spire lore, your Spire is
guaranteed to appear within {FRONTIER.discovery.spireRevealLimit} reveals.

## Fragment caches in ruins

<GameIcon name="fragment" /> Clearing a ruin can also award free Fragments from a hidden cache:
{FRONTIER.caches.surface}
chance on the {FRONTIER.depths[0].name}, {FRONTIER.caches.veil} in the {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, and
{FRONTIER.caches.hollow} in the {FRONTIER.depths[2].name}. A cache pays 1 Fragment per
{FRONTIER.caches.troopsPerFragment} guard troops of that ruin.

## Supply posts

Select any camp you cleared today and pick an adjacent army to reinforce it directly from your home troop stock, paying
the same wheat cost as deploying at home. Supply posts work on the {FRONTIER.depths[0].name}, {FRONTIER.depths[1].name},
and {FRONTIER.depths[2].name} until the day ends.

## The Spire and deeper layers

Spire lore ([Your realm](/frontier/realm#castle-lore)) lets you enter the {FRONTIER.depths[1].name},{" "}
{FRONTIER.depths[2].name}, and the shared [{FRONTIER.names.deep}](/frontier/deep). Select an army beside your Spire and
tap **Enter**. Descending costs no stamina, and the army stays on that layer for the rest of the day.
