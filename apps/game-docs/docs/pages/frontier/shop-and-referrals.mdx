## Fragments

<GameIcon name="fragment" /> **Fragments** are the optional in-game currency for Season 1. Fragments stay on your
account, cannot be sent or withdrawn, and never become LORDS. You do not need Fragments to play, enter the Deep, or earn
LORDS. In a match, click your **Fragments** count on your realm card (desktop) or stores bar (phone) to open the Shop.

- Custom amounts cost {FRONTIER.shop.fragmentPrice} per Fragment, from {FRONTIER.shop.minFragments} up to
  {FRONTIER.shop.dailyCap} Fragments per day. Preset packs are {FRONTIER.shop.packs[0].fragments}
  ({FRONTIER.shop.packs[0].price}), {FRONTIER.shop.packs[1].fragments} ({FRONTIER.shop.packs[1].price}), and
  {FRONTIER.shop.packs[2].fragments} ({FRONTIER.shop.packs[2].price}).
- You pay with {FRONTIER.shop.payTokens} from your linked [Payout wallet](/account/payout-wallet). Non-LORDS tokens swap
  into LORDS through AVNU.

### Free ways to earn Fragments

- Ruins hold either a relic or a Fragment cache ({FRONTIER.caches.surface} on the {FRONTIER.depths[0].name},
  {FRONTIER.caches.veil} in the {FRONTIER.depths[1].name}, {FRONTIER.caches.hollow} in the {FRONTIER.depths[2].name}),
  paying 1 Fragment per {FRONTIER.caches.troopsPerFragment} guard troops.
- A **Wonder Realm NFT** adds +{FRONTIER.quest.wonderFragments} Fragments every time you collect your daily quest.
- Inviting friends to your Aspect pays {FRONTIER.shop.friendFragments} Fragments when a friend reaches
  {FRONTIER.shop.friendGlory} Glory in the Deep.

## Referrals (invite friends)

<GameIcon name="share" /> Once you join an Aspect, copy your personal Aspect link or post it on X from the **Season**
page in the game, or from **You → Referrals & Invite** in the app. Friends who sign up through your link join your
Aspect when creating their realm (an account can have only one inviter). If an invited friend joins with a Realm NFT,
their Realm NFT still sets their Aspect — they can choose to play their Realm NFT or join your Aspect for free without
the Realm NFT quest bonus. Your own accounts on the same browser do not count as referrals.

Each friend who joins through your link and reaches {FRONTIER.shop.friendGlory} Glory in the Deep pays you
{FRONTIER.shop.friendFragments} Fragments, with no cap per season.
