> {FRONTIER.shop.statusNote}

## Fragments

<GameIcon name="fragment" /> **Fragments** are the optional in-game currency for Season 1. You do not need Fragments to
play or enter the Deep.

- Fragments cost {FRONTIER.shop.fragmentCents} cent per Fragment. Packs are {FRONTIER.shop.packs[0].fragments} (
  {FRONTIER.shop.packs[0].price}), {FRONTIER.shop.packs[1].fragments} ({FRONTIER.shop.packs[1].price}), and{" "}
  {FRONTIER.shop.packs[2].fragments} ({FRONTIER.shop.packs[2].price}), or any custom amount from{" "}
  {FRONTIER.shop.minFragments} up to {FRONTIER.shop.dailyCap} Fragments per day.
- You pay with LORDS, ETH, STRK, or USDC from your linked [Payout wallet](/account/payout-wallet). Non-LORDS tokens swap
  into LORDS through AVNU.

### What you can spend Fragments on

| Use                                                 | Cost                                                                  | Rule                        |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| <GameIcon name="final-seal" /> **The Ancient Seal** | {FRONTIER.shop.sealFragments} Fragments ({FRONTIER.shop.sealDollars}) | {FRONTIER.shop.sealRule}    |
| <GameIcon name="stamina" /> **Stamina refill**      | 1 Fragment per {FRONTIER.stamina.perFragment} stamina                 | {FRONTIER.shop.staminaRule} |
| <GameIcon name="shrine" /> **Oath reroll**          | {FRONTIER.oaths.rerollFragments} Fragments                            | {FRONTIER.oaths.rerollNote} |

If your home is already a held Realm, it receives the {FRONTIER.quest.bonusPercent} daily quest labor bonus without an
Ancient Seal.

### Free ways to earn Fragments

- Ruins can drop free Fragment caches ({FRONTIER.caches.surface} on the Surface, {FRONTIER.caches.veil} in the Veil,
  {FRONTIER.caches.hollow} in the Hollow), paying 1 Fragment per {FRONTIER.caches.troopsPerFragment} guard troops.
- A held Wonder Realm adds +{FRONTIER.quest.wonderFragments} Fragments every time you collect your daily quest.
- Inviting friends to your Aspect earns free Fragments as they reach Glory in the Deep.

## Referrals (invite friends)

<GameIcon name="share" /> Once you join an Aspect, open **Invite** to copy your personal Aspect link or share it on X.
Friends who sign up through your link land on your Aspect when creating their realm.

- Each friend who joins through your link and reaches {FRONTIER.shop.friendGlory} Glory in the Deep pays you{" "}
  {FRONTIER.shop.friendFragments} Fragments.
- Up to {FRONTIER.shop.friendsLimit} friends count per season. Reaching your {FRONTIER.shop.friendsLimit}th activated
  friend pays a +{FRONTIER.shop.friendsBonus} Fragment bonus.
