# Oaths

Oaths power up an individual army for the current game day. Every Oath has one flat rule and no levels. Any army of any
Aspect can draw any of the {FRONTIER.oaths.count} Oaths. All Oaths reset when the day ends.

## How you get an Oath pick

There are two ways an army triggers an Oath pick on the {FRONTIER.layers.personalOr}:

1. **<GameIcon name="shrine" /> Reveal a Shrine.** Research **Shrine lore** at your castle to find Shrines
   ({FRONTIER.discovery.shrine} chance per reveal). Revealing a Shrine opens a pick of {FRONTIER.oaths.drawCount} Oaths
   immediately for that army.
2. **<GameIcon name={FRONTIER.beastFamilies[1].icon} /> Clear a Band.** Bands ({FRONTIER.beastFamilies[1].surface},
   {FRONTIER.beastFamilies[1].veil}, {FRONTIER.beastFamilies[1].hollow}; {FRONTIER.discovery.band} chance per reveal)
   award a pick of {FRONTIER.oaths.drawCount} Oaths to the army that lands the last hit.

## Rules of the pick

- **Drawn from what you do not hold:** the contract draws {FRONTIER.oaths.drawCount} distinct Oaths at random from the
  {FRONTIER.oaths.count} Orders that the army does not already hold. An army never holds two copies of the same Oath.
- **One pick at a time:** while an Oath pick is open on an army, that army must choose an Oath, replace an existing one,
  or pass before it can move, reveal, attack, or enter a Spire.
- **Full slots:** when an army's Oath slots are full, picking a new Oath replaces an Oath you choose.
- **Pass for stamina:** passing skips the Oaths and gives that army +{FRONTIER.oaths.passStamina} stamina immediately
  (up to its bar maximum).
- **Reroll:** {FRONTIER.oaths.rerollNote} The redrawn card is always an Oath the army does not hold and that is not
  already shown. See [Shop and referrals](/frontier/shop-and-referrals) for current Shop status.
- **In the Deep and at day's end:** an army keeps its Oaths when it enters [the Deep](/frontier/deep); its Order icons
  show on the site guard card and count in rival fight previews. All Oaths clear when the army ends with the day.

## Oath slots

Every army starts with {FRONTIER.oaths.baseSlots} Oath slots. You can research {FRONTIER.oaths.extraSlots} extra slots
separately for {FRONTIER.troops.knight} (at your {FRONTIER.names.barracks}), {FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman} (at your
{FRONTIER.names.range}), and {FRONTIER.troops.scout} (at your {FRONTIER.names.scoutsLodge}), up to
{FRONTIER.oaths.maxSlots} slots total. Unlocked slots stay for the whole season and apply to every new army of that
troop kind.

| Oath slot                              | Essence                                  | Labor                                  |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| First {FRONTIER.oaths.baseSlots} slots | Free                                     | Free                                   |
| {FRONTIER.research.oathSlots[0].slot}  | {FRONTIER.research.oathSlots[0].essence} | {FRONTIER.research.oathSlots[0].labor} |

| {FRONTIER.research.oathSlots[2].slot}  | {FRONTIER.research.oathSlots[2].essence} | {FRONTIER.research.oathSlots[2].labor} |

## Strong Oath pairs

No single set of Oaths is best at every job. Match your Oaths to what that army will do today: breaking a rival Deep
site, holding a Deep slot, clearing camps on the {FRONTIER.layers.personalOr}, or exploring for Spires and ruins.
