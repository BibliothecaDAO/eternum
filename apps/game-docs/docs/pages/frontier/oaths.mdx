# Oaths

Oaths improve an individual army for the current game day. Every Oath has one fixed rule and no levels. Any army of any
Aspect can be offered any of the {FRONTIER.oaths.count} Oaths. All Oaths reset when the day ends.

## How you get an Oath pick

There are two ways an army triggers an Oath pick on the {FRONTIER.layers.personalOr}:

1. **<GameIcon name="shrine" /> Reveal a shrine.** Research **Shrine lore** at your castle to find shrines
   ({FRONTIER.discovery.shrine} chance per reveal). Revealing a shrine opens a pick of {FRONTIER.oaths.drawCount} Oaths
   immediately for that army.
2. **<GameIcon name={FRONTIER.beastFamilies[1].icon} /> Clear a band.** Bands — {FRONTIER.oaths.bandSummary}
   ({FRONTIER.discovery.band} chance per reveal) — award a pick of {FRONTIER.oaths.drawCount} Oaths to the army that
   lands the last hit.

## Rules of the pick

- **Offered from what you do not hold:** each pick offers {FRONTIER.oaths.drawCount} distinct Oaths at random from the
  {FRONTIER.oaths.count} Oaths that the army does not already hold. An army never holds two copies of the same Oath.
- **One pick at a time:** while an Oath pick is open on an army, that army must choose an Oath, replace an existing one,
  or pass before it can move, reveal, attack, or enter a Spire.
- **Full slots:** when an army's Oath slots are full, picking a new Oath replaces an Oath you choose.
- **Pass for stamina:** passing skips the offered Oaths and gives that army +{FRONTIER.oaths.passStamina} stamina
  immediately (up to its bar maximum).
- **Reroll:** {FRONTIER.oaths.rerollNote} The replacement Oath is always an Oath the army does not hold and that is not
  already offered. See [Shop and referrals](/frontier/shop-and-referrals) for current Shop status.
- **In the Deep and at day's end:** an army keeps its Oaths when it enters [the Deep](/frontier/deep); its Order icons
  show on the site panel and count in rival fight previews. All Oaths clear when the army ends with the day.

## Oath slots

Every army starts with {FRONTIER.oaths.baseSlots} Oath slots. You can research {FRONTIER.oaths.extraSlots} extra slots
separately for {FRONTIER.troops.knight} (at your {FRONTIER.names.barracks}), {FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman} (at your
{FRONTIER.names.range}), and {FRONTIER.troops.scout} (at your {FRONTIER.names.scoutsLodge}), up to
{FRONTIER.oaths.maxSlots} slots total. Unlocked slots stay for the whole season and apply to every new army of that
troop kind.

### How key Oaths work

- **Fury:** refunds {FRONTIER.oaths.furyPercent} of the winning attack's cost only — {FRONTIER.oaths.furyRefundBase}
  stamina on a normal attack, or {FRONTIER.oaths.furyRefundAnger} stamina with Anger. Earlier attacks on the same target
  are not refunded ({FRONTIER.oaths.furyExampleAttacks} attacks on a camp cost {FRONTIER.oaths.furyExampleTotal} stamina
  and refund {FRONTIER.oaths.furyRefundBase}, not {FRONTIER.oaths.furyExampleWrong}).
- **Skill and Fury together:** Skill checks the army's stamina after paying the attack cost and before any Fury refund.
  An army attacking from {FRONTIER.oaths.skillExampleStart} stamina drops to {FRONTIER.oaths.skillExampleAfter} (below
  {FRONTIER.oaths.skillThreshold}), gains Skill's {FRONTIER.oaths.skillDamagePercent} damage, and if that attack clears
  the target, Fury refunds {FRONTIER.oaths.furyRefundBase} stamina so the army ends on {FRONTIER.oaths.skillExampleEnd}.
- **Twins:** does not stack across multiple adjacent armies. It checks the six tiles around the attacking army, picks
  the single largest living army of the **same owner** from the same day (a teammate's army never counts), and adds
  {FRONTIER.oaths.twinsPercent} of **that adjacent army's** damage against the target with no Oaths and no stamina cost
  on the helper.
- **Titans and Logistics:** Titans adds a flat {FRONTIER.oaths.titansBonus} to the stamina bar on top of Logistics (it
  does not double the bar and is not capped at {FRONTIER.oaths.titansBaseMax}). Logistics scales only the base
  {FRONTIER.stamina.max} bar ({FRONTIER.stamina.logisticsStepAmount} per rank), so an army with Titans holds
  {FRONTIER.oaths.titansBaseMax} max stamina at Logistics 0 up to {FRONTIER.oaths.titansLogisticsMax} at Logistics V,
  and gains {FRONTIER.oaths.titansBonus} stamina immediately up to the new maximum.
