# Aspects and rewards

Every realm in Frontier Season 1 belongs to one of {FRONTIER.aspectsCount} **Aspects**. Together, an Aspect's players
race to raise their Aspect's **hyperstructure** in [the Deep](/frontier/deep) and share LORDS rewards at the end of the
season.

## The {FRONTIER.aspectsCount} Aspects and their {FRONTIER.oaths.count} Orders

If you create your realm with a **Realm NFT** (a Realms collection NFT held in your linked payout wallet), your Aspect
is fixed by that Realm NFT's Order. If you start with a free realm, you pick your Aspect and an Order inside it when you
enter. No Aspect is ever closed, you cannot switch Aspects mid-season, and all {FRONTIER.oaths.count} Orders grant their
[Oaths](/frontier/oaths) to every army regardless of Aspect.

## The hyperstructure race

<GameIcon name="hyperstructure" /> Each Aspect builds its own hyperstructure by holding **Hyperstructure archives** in
the Deep. Hyperstructures are tracked as a race counter rather than a tile on the map, so they cannot be attacked
directly.

- Each hyperstructure has {FRONTIER.race.stages} stages of {FRONTIER.race.stageHeight} height each, so
  {FRONTIER.race.finishHeight} height wins the season immediately.
- Reaching stages 1 through 4 activates a **Stage Triumph** for your whole Aspect on the next game day
  ([Banes and Triumphs](/frontier/banes#stage-triumphs)).
- The season lasts {FRONTIER.day.seasonDays} game days at most. If no Aspect finishes by day {FRONTIER.day.seasonDays},
  nobody receives the finish bonus, and every Aspect still earns LORDS for the height it built.
- While a rival Team Bane is active on your Aspect, your hyperstructure stops gaining height until your Aspect ends the
  bane, though Archive guards still earn Glory ([Banes and Triumphs](/frontier/banes#rival-team-banes)).

Open **Aspect Race** in the game to follow all four hyperstructures, their stages, active Stage Triumphs, your Aspect's
bane bar charge, your Aspect's members ranked by Glory (and which Deep site they guard), and recent fights in the Deep.

## Glory and LORDS rewards

<GameIcon name="lords" /> Season 1 pays up to {FRONTIER.race.maxSeasonLords} LORDS in total. Each Aspect's pool depends
on its own hyperstructure height when the season ends:

- Every Aspect earns {FRONTIER.race.lordsPerHeight} LORDS per height point reached, regardless of what other Aspects
  built.
- The first Aspect to reach {FRONTIER.race.finishHeight} height wins a +{FRONTIER.race.finishBonus} LORDS finish bonus
  (up to {FRONTIER.race.maxFinishLords} LORDS for that Aspect).
- LORDS for unbuilt height and any unearned finish bonus return to the treasury when the season closes.
- Inside each Aspect, the pool splits by **Glory** share among members who earned at least {FRONTIER.race.gloryToShare}
  Glory during the season, with no Realm NFT, ticket, Seal, or per-player cap required.
- You earn Glory only by holding **Hyperstructure archives** and **Bane altars** in [the Deep](/frontier/deep). Archive
  Glory goes only to the guards holding that Archive (split by troop count), while the whole Aspect gains the
  hyperstructure height. Essence cores, Ethereal mines, and beaten troops give no Glory.

Your linked [Payout wallet](/account/payout-wallet) receives your LORDS after the season ends.
