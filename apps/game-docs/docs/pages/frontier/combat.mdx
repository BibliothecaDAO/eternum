# Combat

Combat uses a fixed formula with no random rolls, so the fight preview is exact before you commit. Terrain gives no
combat bonus or penalty. Guards keep their losses and never recover troops, so two or more armies can weaken a strong
guard across several attacks.

Nobody can attack your realm or your armies on the {FRONTIER.layers.personalOr}. Player-against-player fights happen
only in [the Deep](/frontier/deep), and allies of the same Aspect can never attack each other.

## The combat formula

Each attack costs {FRONTIER.stamina.attack} stamina ({FRONTIER.oaths.angerAttack} with the Anger Oath). Extra stamina
above {FRONTIER.stamina.attack} adds no damage; defenders need {FRONTIER.stamina.defenseReq} stamina and take no
low-stamina penalty. The only stamina-linked damage effect is the Skill Oath, which adds
{FRONTIER.oaths.skillDamagePercent} damage when the attacking army has less than {FRONTIER.oaths.skillThreshold} stamina
left after paying the attack cost ([Oaths](/frontier/oaths#how-key-oaths-work)).

In each attack, each side starts with base damage:

**{FRONTIER.combat.formula}**

Here the damage factor is {FRONTIER.combat.damageFactor} and the combined-troops exponent is {FRONTIER.combat.beta}.
Then four modifiers apply:

1. **Troop kind:** {FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman} and {FRONTIER.troops.scout} deal
   {FRONTIER.attributes.lightDamageReduction} less damage than {FRONTIER.troops.knight}.
2. **Deep guard protection:** {FRONTIER.troops.knight} holding a Deep site slot take
   {FRONTIER.attributes.knightGuardReduction} less damage. Wild beasts never get this reduction.
3. **Active relics:** a Blade relic adds to damage dealt and a Ward relic lowers damage taken
   ([Relics](/frontier/relics)).
4. **Oaths:** combat Oaths (Power, Skill, Giants, Protection) multiply together; the Twins adds
   {FRONTIER.oaths.twinsPercent} of the largest adjacent own army's damage against the target
   ([Oaths](/frontier/oaths#how-key-oaths-work)).

## Blade vs bow

Every troop and every guard carries a **mark** on its map token and portrait: either **blade** or **bow**.

| Troop                             | Mark  | Damage dealt                                                                  | Special                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------- | ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **{FRONTIER.troops.knight}**      | Blade | **Full damage**                                                               | Takes **{FRONTIER.attributes.knightGuardReduction} less damage** while garrisoned in a Deep site |
| **{FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman}** | Bow   | **{FRONTIER.attributes.lightDamageReduction} less** than {FRONTIER.troops.knight} | **Strikes first** against Blade enemies                                                       |
| **{FRONTIER.troops.scout}**       | Blade | **{FRONTIER.attributes.lightDamageReduction} less** than {FRONTIER.troops.knight} | Reveals tiles for **{FRONTIER.stamina.scoutReveal}** stamina instead of {FRONTIER.stamina.reveal} |

| Attacker | Defender | Who strikes first                                           |
| -------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bow      | Blade    | **Attacker (bow)** — defender hits back only with survivors |
| Blade    | Bow      | **Defender (bow)** — attacker hits back only with survivors |
| Bow      | Bow      | **Simultaneous** — both sides hit at the same time          |
| Blade    | Blade    | **Simultaneous** — both sides hit at the same time          |

Because of first strike, bring **{FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman}** (bow) against blade enemies (herds, camps, ruins,
{FRONTIER.combat.bladeDeepBeasts}) and **{FRONTIER.troops.knight}** (blade) against bow enemies (bands, rifts,
{FRONTIER.combat.bowDeepBeasts}). As a rule of thumb, clearing a guard with no Oaths or relics takes about
{FRONTIER.combat.exampleRatio} the guard's troops with the matching mark.

## Worked example

Suppose you attack a {FRONTIER.combat.exampleLabel} ({FRONTIER.combat.exampleGuard} blade troops) with a
{FRONTIER.combat.exampleRatio} army ({FRONTIER.combat.exampleSent} troops) and no Oaths or relics:

- **{FRONTIER.combat.exampleSent} {FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman} (bow vs blade):** they shoot first on each attack, clear
  the camp in {FRONTIER.combat.exampleCrossbowsExchanges} attacks, lose {FRONTIER.combat.exampleCrossbowsLost} troops,
  and keep **{FRONTIER.combat.exampleCrossbowsKept}**.
- **{FRONTIER.combat.exampleSent} {FRONTIER.troops.knight} (blade vs blade):** both sides hit together on each attack,
  clearing the camp in {FRONTIER.combat.exampleKnightsExchanges} attacks, losing {FRONTIER.combat.exampleKnightsLost}
  troops, and keeping **{FRONTIER.combat.exampleKnightsKept}**.
