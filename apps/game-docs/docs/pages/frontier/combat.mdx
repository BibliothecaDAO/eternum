# Combat

Combat uses a fixed formula with no random rolls, so the fight preview is exact before you commit. Terrain gives no
combat bonus or penalty. Guards keep their losses and never recover troops, so two or more armies can weaken a strong
guard across several attacks.

Nobody can attack your realm or your armies on the {FRONTIER.layers.personalOr}. Player-against-player fights happen
only in [the Deep](/frontier/deep), and allies of the same Aspect can never attack each other.

## The combat formula

Each attack costs {FRONTIER.stamina.attack} stamina ({FRONTIER.oaths.angerAttack} with the Anger Oath). Extra stamina
above {FRONTIER.stamina.attack} adds no damage; defenders need {FRONTIER.stamina.defenseReq} stamina and take no
low-stamina penalty. The only stamina-linked damage effect is the Skill Oath, which adds
{FRONTIER.oaths.skillDamagePercent} damage when the attacking army has less than {FRONTIER.oaths.skillThreshold} stamina
left after paying the attack cost ([Oaths](/frontier/oaths#how-key-oaths-work)).

In each attack, each side starts with base damage:

**{FRONTIER.combat.formula}**

Here the damage factor is {FRONTIER.combat.damageFactor} and the combined-troops exponent is {FRONTIER.combat.beta}.
Then four modifiers apply:

1. **Troop kind:** {FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman} and {FRONTIER.troops.scout} deal
   {FRONTIER.attributes.lightDamageReduction} less damage than {FRONTIER.troops.knight}.
2. **Deep guard protection:** {FRONTIER.troops.knight} holding a Deep site slot take
   {FRONTIER.attributes.knightGuardReduction} less damage. Wild beasts never get this reduction.
3. **Active relics:** a Blade relic adds to damage dealt and a Ward relic lowers damage taken
   ([Relics](/frontier/relics)).
4. **Oaths:** combat Oaths (Power, Skill, Giants, Protection) multiply together; the Twins adds
   {FRONTIER.oaths.twinsPercent} of the largest adjacent own army's damage against the target
   ([Oaths](/frontier/oaths#how-key-oaths-work)).

## Melee vs ranged

Every troop and every guard carries a **mark** on its map token and portrait: either **melee** or **ranged**.

| Troop                             | Mark   | Damage dealt                                                                      | Special                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **{FRONTIER.troops.knight}**      | Melee  | **Full damage**                                                                   | Takes **{FRONTIER.attributes.knightGuardReduction} less damage** while guarding a Deep site       |
| **{FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman}** | Ranged | **{FRONTIER.attributes.lightDamageReduction} less** than {FRONTIER.troops.knight} | **Strikes first** against Melee enemies                                                           |
| **{FRONTIER.troops.scout}**       | Melee  | **{FRONTIER.attributes.lightDamageReduction} less** than {FRONTIER.troops.knight} | Reveals tiles for **{FRONTIER.stamina.scoutReveal}** stamina instead of {FRONTIER.stamina.reveal} |

| Attacker | Defender | Who strikes first                                              |
| -------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ranged   | Melee    | **Attacker (ranged)** — defender hits back only with survivors |
| Melee    | Ranged   | **Defender (ranged)** — attacker hits back only with survivors |
| Ranged   | Ranged   | **Simultaneous** — both sides hit at the same time             |
| Melee    | Melee    | **Simultaneous** — both sides hit at the same time             |

**{FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman}** (ranged) are the best general-purpose attackers against almost everything: they strike
first against melee enemies (herds, camps, ruins, {FRONTIER.combat.bladeDeepBeasts}) to clear with far fewer losses
despite dealing {FRONTIER.attributes.lightDamageReduction} less damage than {FRONTIER.troops.knight}, and they hit at
the same time against ranged enemies (bands, rifts, {FRONTIER.combat.bowDeepBeasts}) so they never take a free first
strike, even in even-numbered fights. **{FRONTIER.troops.knight}** (melee) deal full damage and are built for holding
Deep sites (taking {FRONTIER.attributes.knightGuardReduction} less damage from every attack while guarding). Sending
{FRONTIER.troops.knight} against ranged enemies means eating the defender's first strike first — melee gets wrecked
against ranged in even-sized fights, and {FRONTIER.troops.knight} only edge ahead of {FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman}
against ranged defenders when you bring a clear numbers advantage (like the {FRONTIER.combat.exampleRatio} winning
fights shown in the table below, where full damage finally outpaces the ranged first strike).
**{FRONTIER.troops.scout}** (melee) also deal {FRONTIER.attributes.lightDamageReduction} less damage with no first
strike — use them to reveal mist cheaply ({FRONTIER.stamina.scoutReveal} stamina), not for heavy fights. As a rule of
thumb, bringing about {FRONTIER.combat.exampleRatio} the guard's troops clears a site cleanly with no Oaths or relics.

## Worked example

Suppose you attack a {FRONTIER.combat.exampleLabel} ({FRONTIER.combat.exampleGuard} melee troops) with a
{FRONTIER.combat.exampleRatio} army ({FRONTIER.combat.exampleSent} troops) and no Oaths or relics:

- **{FRONTIER.combat.exampleSent} {FRONTIER.troops.crossbowman} (ranged vs melee):** they shoot first on each attack,
  clear the camp in {FRONTIER.combat.exampleCrossbowsExchanges} attacks, lose {FRONTIER.combat.exampleCrossbowsLost}
  troops, and keep **{FRONTIER.combat.exampleCrossbowsKept}**.
- **{FRONTIER.combat.exampleSent} {FRONTIER.troops.knight} (melee vs melee):** both sides hit together on each attack,
  clearing the camp in {FRONTIER.combat.exampleKnightsExchanges} attacks, losing {FRONTIER.combat.exampleKnightsLost}
  troops, and keeping **{FRONTIER.combat.exampleKnightsKept}**.
