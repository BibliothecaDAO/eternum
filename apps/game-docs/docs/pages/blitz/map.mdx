# The map

## The layout

The map starts dark. At the start everyone sees every realm with the tiles around it, and every Hyperstructure site.
Once a tile is explored, everyone sees it.

Hyperstructure sites stand in rings around the centre of the map, {BLITZ.map.ringStep} hexes apart, and the number of
rings grows with the number of players in the game:

<HyperstructureRings />

Each player's three realms stand close together, three hexes apart, among the rings.

## Exploring

An army beside an unexplored tile can explore it, for {BLITZ.stamina.explore} stamina. The tile is revealed to everyone,
scores {BLITZ.points.tile} points, and pays the army's home realm one of the finds below: a share of the army's strength
(its troop count times its tier's strength), counted up to {BLITZ.map.exploreCap}. An Exploration Reward relic adds its
bonus on top.

<ExplorationRewards />

<Video name="blitz/explore" caption="An army explores, and each tile it reveals is drawn as it lands." />

A revealed tile can also hold something:

| Find            | Chance per reveal     |
| --------------- | --------------------- |
| A dormant spire | {BLITZ.map.spireFind} |
| An Essence Rift | {BLITZ.map.riftFind}  |
| A camp          | {BLITZ.map.campFind}  |

**Dormant spires.** The finder's realm receives {BLITZ.map.spireEssence} Essence once, plus any active Exploration
Reward relic bonus. The spire then stands on its tile for the rest of the game, blocking it.

## Relic crates

When a player explores and no crate has appeared for {BLITZ.map.crateMinutes} minutes, a relic crate appears within{" "}
{BLITZ.map.crateHexes} hexes of the explored tile, where everyone can see it. An army beside it opens it: its pack of
three relics is revealed, the army keeps the army relics, and the realm relics go to that army's home realm. Opening a
crate scores {BLITZ.points.crate} points.

## Camps, Essence Rifts and Hyperstructures

Each is held by bandits until a player clears its guard and claims it ([Armies and combat](/blitz/armies#capturing)).

- **Camps** start with one T1 guard of {BLITZ.map.lightGuard} troops. A claimed camp holds {BLITZ.map.camp.wheat} wheat,
  {BLITZ.map.camp.labor} labor and {BLITZ.map.camp.donkeys} donkeys, makes {BLITZ.map.campLaborPerSecond} labor a
  second, runs production, and fields one army.
- **Essence Rifts** start with one T1 guard of {BLITZ.map.lightGuard} troops. A held rift makes{" "}
  {BLITZ.map.riftPerSecond} Essence a second, {BLITZ.map.riftTotal} in all.
- **Hyperstructures** are founded by any player on a site: double-click the site, or press **Create here** on its panel.
  It rises guarded by three T2 bandit guards of {BLITZ.map.heavyGuard} troops each. Claiming it scores points once, and
  holding it scores points every second ([Points, results and prizes](/blitz/scoring)).
