# Troop Tiers

<img src="/images/covers/drake-rider.png" alt="Drake Rider" className="w-full rounded-lg my-6" />

Troops in Blitz come in three distinct tiers: T1, T2, and T3. Tier 1 (T1) represents basic troops, while T2 and T3
troops are more advanced, offering significantly increased combat strength and stamina.

Higher-tier troops are produced by constructing specialized buildings that require rare resources and lower-tier troops
as inputs:

- Two T1 troops combine to produce one T2 troop
- Two T2 troops combine to produce one T3 troop

Each troop tier provides escalating advantages in terms of damage output and stamina.

## Combat Roles

- **Knights** are adjacent-only structure specialists, with stronger assaults into structure guards and sturdier guard
  defense when garrisoned.
- **Crossbowmen** can attack from 2 hexes away. Their adjacent attacks are normal combat; their ranged attacks are safer
  but damage-inefficient pokes.
- **Paladins** remain high-mobility cavalry whose strengths depend heavily on map position and biome distribution.
