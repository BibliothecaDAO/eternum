# Game Results

Free Blitz slots have no entry fee or prize pool. After the game ends, hyperstructure points are settled in bounded
batches and the final result records every roster member's rank and victory points.

Equal scores share a competition rank. A tie for first takes ranks 1 and 1; the next player is rank 3. Players who
finish with zero points share last place, including registered players who never acted.

The result becomes final only when the complete roster has been recorded. Interrupted finalization resumes from its
recorded progress. The leaderboard and game review show these native results.

There are no chest awards, MMR updates or token payouts in the current free playtest flow.
