# Relics

Relics are powerful consumables that can be discovered in Blitz from
[Relic Crates](/blitz/worldmap-movement/worldmap#relic-crates). Depending on the nature of a relic, it can be consumed
by an army or a Realm in exchange for a time-limited bonus, such as a damage or production boost. When utilized at the
right time, these items can provide a strategic edge over your opponents.

Timed relic effects last **3 Eternum Days (18 minutes)**.

There are currently nine relic types, each with two levels. A level 2 relic is simply a more powerful version of the
level 1 counterpart. Every relic type has a distinct effect, which determines whether it can be activated by a Realm or
an army. To activate a relic and gain the desired effect, the army or Realm must pay an amount of Essence. These details

Each Relic Crate holds one themed pack of three relics, chosen when the crate appears, so a crate's tile names its pack
before anyone opens it. The army that opens the crate receives all three.

_Note: Relics are still under development. Initial versions of Blitz will limit their scope to within the game instance.

out for announcements in the Realms Discord relating to relics. This page will be updated as more information is made
available to the community._
