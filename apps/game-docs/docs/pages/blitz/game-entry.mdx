# Game Entry

Sign in with your Realms identity and register for a free Blitz slot before registration closes. Your gameplay account
is prepared through the existing sign-in flow. Registration requires no token payment or season pass.

When registration closes, the roster is fixed and split into balanced Regular Blitz games of up to 24 players. For
example, 25 players form games of 13 and 12. Each player belongs to one game; registration order determines grouping.

## Automatic settlement

The game shuffles its fixed roster and settles three realms per player, with provisions and a starting Delta guard.
There is no manual settlement race. Play starts only after every player's realms are ready; everyone gets the same
scheduled game duration. Realm names come from their realm IDs.

Your slot shows your assigned game. Enter through your game card once it is ready. A one-player slot creates a
one-player game; an empty slot creates no game. Duel is outside this playtest flow.

## Results

At the end, the game finishes hyperstructure point settlement and records every player's final victory points and rank.
Ties share a competition rank: scores of 100, 100 and 50 rank 1, 1 and 3. Players with zero VP tie last.

These free playtests award no chests, token payouts or MMR changes.
