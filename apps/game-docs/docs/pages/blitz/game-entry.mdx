# Game Entry

Free Blitz slots close every day at 11:00 and 20:00 UTC. The next slot opens as soon as the previous one closes, so
there is always one to register for. Sign in with your Realms identity and register before the slot closes; no token
payment or season pass is needed, and nobody schedules the slot by hand.

When the slot closes, its roster is fixed and split into Regular Blitz games of up to 24 players. For example, 25
players form games of 13 and 12. Each player belongs to one game; registration order determines grouping.

## Automatic settlement

The game shuffles its fixed roster and settles three realms per player, with provisions and a starting Delta guard.
There is no manual settlement race. Play starts only after every player's realms are ready; everyone gets the same
scheduled game duration. Realm names come from their realm IDs.

Your slot shows your assigned game until the next slot closes. Opening the game before it is ready shows your realms
being prepared, with the roster's progress; a player who is not on the roster can only spectate. A one-player slot
creates a one-player game; an empty slot creates no game. Duel has no free slot flow.

## Results

At the end, the game finishes hyperstructure point settlement and records every player's final victory points and rank.
Ties share a competition rank: scores of 100, 100 and 50 rank 1, 1 and 3. Players with zero VP tie last.

Free slots award no chests or token payouts.
