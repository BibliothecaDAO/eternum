# Armies and combat

An army holds one troop type at one tier. Each hex holds one army.

## Starting forces

Each of your three realms is dealt one troop type, one realm Knights, one Crossbowmen and one Paladins, in an order
drawn for you. Each starts with {startingForcesOf("blitz").garrison} T1 troops of its type garrisoned and{" "}
{startingForcesOf("blitz").reserve} in reserve. No army stands on the map at the start: create one from the reserve.

Barracks make more: T1 troops from wheat and a resource, T2 from T1 troops, T3 from T2
([Realms and production](/blitz/realms#production)).

## Tiers and deployment

A troop's strength is its count times its tier's strength: {BLITZ.troops.strength[0]} for T1,{" "}
{BLITZ.troops.strength[1]} for T2, {BLITZ.troops.strength[2]} for T3. A realm's level caps the strength of one army
([Realms and production](/blitz/realms#levels)), and the largest army is that cap divided by its tier's strength,
times{" "} {BLITZ.troops.sizeModifier[0]} for T1, {BLITZ.troops.sizeModifier[1]} for T2 and
{BLITZ.troops.sizeModifier[2]} for T3.

**Field armies** stand on the map. Select your realm and right-click a free tile beside it to deploy one there, or use
the Military window (`M`). A new army starts with {BLITZ.stamina.start} stamina.

**Guards** stand in a structure's guard slots and do not appear on the map. An attacker meets them one slot at a time. A
slot whose guard falls cannot take a new one for {BLITZ.troops.guardRefillMinutes} minutes. A guard can attack an army
beside its structure.

## Merging and moving troops

Select an army, then right-click another of your armies or one of your structures beside it: the transfer panel moves
troops between them, across your realms too. Both sides must hold the same troop type and tier; an empty guard slot
takes any type and tier, and only from an army. A guard cannot move straight to another guard slot. The army that
receives troops keeps the lower of the two staminas and the longer of the two battle timers.

## Stamina

A T1 army holds {BLITZ.stamina.max} stamina, a T2 army {BLITZ.stamina.t2} and a T3 army {BLITZ.stamina.t3}. Each
recovers {BLITZ.stamina.perTick} every {BLITZ.game.tickSeconds} seconds. Exploring a tile costs {BLITZ.stamina.explore}.
A step onto a known tile costs {BLITZ.stamina.step}, {BLITZ.stamina.biomeStep} more or less by biome and troop type.
Moving and exploring also cost {BLITZ.stamina.wheatPerTroop} wheat a troop, from the army's home realm.

## Attacking

Select an army and right-click an enemy within reach: the attack forecast opens with each side's losses, and **Attack**
sends it. Attacking takes {BLITZ.stamina.attack} stamina; without it, the attack is refused. The defender spends{" "}
{BLITZ.stamina.defense} stamina, and deals {BLITZ.troops.lowStaminaPenalty} less damage without it.

## Troop roles

- **Knights** deal {BLITZ.troops.knightAssault} more damage attacking a structure's guards from beside it, and as guards
  take {BLITZ.troops.knightGuard} less damage.
- **Crossbowmen** can attack from {BLITZ.troops.crossbowRange} hexes away. A ranged attack still costs the attacker{" "}
  {BLITZ.stamina.attack} stamina, but the target spends only {BLITZ.stamina.rangedDefense} and strikes no blow back. It
  deals {BLITZ.troops.rangedOnArmies} of its damage to an army and {BLITZ.troops.rangedOnGuards} to a structure's
  guards, ignores biomes, and its battle timer is half as long. A ranged attack never claims a structure.
- **Paladins** have no combat bonus. Their biome bonuses and maluses are the largest, and open ground costs them the
  least stamina.

## Capturing

When a structure's last guard falls, an army beside it claims it. Realms, camps, Essence Rifts and Hyperstructures can
all be captured, and a captured realm's armies go with it. A player who loses their last realm watches the rest of the
game.
