# Ethereal depth medallions

`ethereal-1.svg`, `ethereal-2.svg` and `ethereal-3.svg` are the editable vector sources and runtime assets. All shapes
are stored directly in their SVG paths, on a 96-unit canvas. No fonts, raster masters, filters or build step are
required.

The shared motif is a stone threshold around an ink-black swirling portal. Ethereal I has a single parchment arch;
Ethereal II adds gold flanking pylons and a taller inner arch; Ethereal III has a pointed gold crown, three bright
keystones and a deeper double curl. The silhouettes and ornament count distinguish the depths without relying on text.

Direction: Frontier design section 3.12 and the three-portal research chain in mockup 3. These are flat ink-and-gold
medallions: `#dfaa54` gold, `#eadfc8` parchment, `#1b1207` ink. The dark circular backing is part of the illustration;
the surrounding learned, open or locked status ring belongs to the frontend. Transparent corners leave that ring clear.

Runtime paths: `/images/frontier/depths/ethereal-{1,2,3}.svg`. Designed for 64–80 px research medallions; depth names
and accessible labels come from the surrounding UI. The review page shows both sizes on the dark HUD, with captures
outside the repository. No research tree wiring is included in this asset group.
