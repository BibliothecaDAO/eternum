## All modes

### New

- {lords} **Real LORDS**: play.realms.party pays in LORDS from the Devcon season on: Blitz entries, chests, prizes and
  Frontier's LORDS. The dev game keeps its free test LORDS.
- {home} **Choose your home**: Entering a Frontier season, the home you found stands large with what it claims each day,
  your other choices beside it and every Realm a search away. Found it with a Realm your wallet holds, an event ticket
  or a free home; no Realm number is typed any more.
- {person} **Holdings on You**: Your card on You shows the Realms your wallet holds and your Devcon ticket, each opening
  its sheet. With no wallet linked, it reads Link a wallet for your Realms and LORDS.
- {menu} **Four places**: Play, Season, Chat and You on the bar, phone and desktop alike; What's new and Market are rows
  in You. A mode with nothing to play now is dimmed and says why.
- {chat} **Chat rebuilt**: One Global room everywhere, in the app, Frontier and Blitz, with private conversations in
  their own Messages tab. Chat opens on the newest message, replies quote the line they answer, @ mentions a player, a
  name opens the player's card with Message and Mute, and a place on the map drops in as a chip that opens it.
- {notifications} **Push notifications**: Your phone can tell you when a Frontier day opens and what you can claim, when
  the season is over, when a Blitz settles, and when a message or a mention reaches you. Nothing between 22:00 and 08:00
  on your phone's clock.
- {layers} **The Realms brand**: The lettered REALMS roundel in the top bar, new mode colours and lockups, new icons and
  a new favicon.
- {move} **Army paths**: The selected army's route draws as chevrons to a ringed destination, a hovered army shows a
  slim arrow, and Move or Explore previews its path before you order it.

### Fixes

- {play} **A smooth start**: The loading screen holds until the opening view is ready, so the first second in a game no
  longer stutters.
- {map} **The camera stays put**: Fighting, exploring, moving or building no longer move the camera; picking an army
  from a list centres on it.
- {explore} **Results sooner**: Claims, moves and your own explores show their result on screen sooner.
- {map} **Red on the minimap**: Enemies are red on the minimap, as on the map's labels.
- {map-army} **Labels at every zoom**: Hover labels open at every zoom, a beast's label stays beside your army, and
  cleared sites lose their flag token.
- {realm} **Realm view light**: Every building in the realm view is lit one way in any weather, and its shadows agree
  with the trees'.

## Frontier

### New

- {star} **Skill points**: XP only goes up now. At 75, 200, 400, 800 and 1,500 XP an army earns a Common, Uncommon,
  Rare, Epic and Legendary skill point, then the same again every 1,500. A point raises one attribute one rank, up to
  the point's own rank, and refills 20 to 60 stamina by its rank; spent on Stamina alone it refills double.
- {research} **Ranks I to V**: Every attribute and every research side starts at 0 and climbs Common I to Legendary V.
  Research gains a cheap first rank at half the old first price, and training buildings grant new armies rank I.
- {attack} **Battle**: Each rank hits harder: 10%, 25%, 45%, 70%, then 100%.
- {stamina} **Logistics**: Each rank carries more stamina: 20, 45, 75, 110, then 150.
- {explore} **Scouting**: Choose Trolls, Rifts or Sanctuaries (Shrines and Wells together), and each rank adds 10%, 15%,
  20%, 25%, then 30% to that kind's find rate: all five on one kind find twice as many.
- {people} **Homecoming**: Each rank sends more survivors home at the day's end: 3%, 8%, 14%, 21%, then 30%.
- {shrine} **Shrines grant a skill point**: A Shrine holds one skill point, rolled when it is found and shown on its
  card: on the surface Common 50%, Uncommon 30%, Rare 15%, Epic 4% and Legendary 1%, one rank higher for each depth
  below. An army beside it takes the point.
- {explore} **Explore XP by depth**: A reveal gives 2, 3, 4 or 5 XP, by depth. `2 → 2–5`
- {devcon-realm} **The Devcon season**: Frontier at Devcon, from Nov 3 12:00 IST to Nov 6 20:00 IST, at three times the
  speed: game days of 4 to 12 hours, with stamina and production three times as fast.
- {devcon-golden-realm} **Devcon tickets**: Each flyer founds a Devcon realm that claims 1,000 Labor and 1,000 Essence a
  game day; 15 golden tickets claim 2,000 and 2,000. One ticket per account, and Devcon realms have their own look.
- {victory} **300,000 LORDS at Devcon**: 70% pays chests, and the leaderboard's top 3 take 45,000, 30,000 and 15,000.
- {leaderboard} **The season's podium**: 70% of every season's pool pays chests and 30% its leaderboard's top places,
  shown as medal tiles in the game and the app. Only realms that cleared a site take a place.
- {lords} **Paid at the season's end**: The LORDS your realm still holds when a season ends, and your prize, are paid to
  your linked wallet automatically. Withdrawals during the season stay instant.
- {lords} **Link a wallet for your LORDS**: With LORDS and no linked wallet, your purse, Withdraw, the season's end and
  a ruin's chest say so, with Link wallet. After the season, link one by the claim deadline, 7 days on, or those LORDS
  go to the next season.
- {claim} **Realm Labor**: Claim your home's Labor, and a ticket's Essence, once a game day. The sheet shows the days
  around today, what fits in your storage, and Remind me for the next day.
- {chest} **The ruin's chest**: Clearing a ruin opens a real chest in the middle of the screen, its LORDS counting up.
- {deploy} **Deploy into a slot**: Choose the army slot Deploy fills, starting on your first open one. Deploy fits a
  phone on one screen, lights only the tiles it can use, and puts Build beside it when wheat limits it.
- {lords} **Partial refill**: Refill buys as much stamina as your LORDS cover, 1 LORDS a point.
- {eye} **Visit a realm**: Tap any building or the castle of a realm you visit to see what it is and makes. On a phone,
  a double tap on a realm opens its view, as a double-click does on a desktop.
- {chronicles} **One row per action**: The Feed tells a won attack and the clear it made as one row, and shows a clear's
  XP beside what it paid. On a desktop the Feed is a panel like Season and Research.
- {attack} **Forecast from afar**: A site's card shows the fight's forecast even when your army is too far away, with
  Move when it can get there.
- {research} **Research says what it does**: Building cards and Research spell out what each rank gives, and why a
  building's price rose.

### Balance

- {beast-cyclops} **Beasts pay labor**: Clearing beasts pays labor, a quarter of their starting troops, as well as XP.
- {ruin} **Ruins**: Find any number of ruins and clear one a day; the others disappear.
- {chest} **Chests at every depth**: A chest a reveal finds can turn up at any depth, with its layer's chest odds, and
  deeper ruins pay richer chests: Ethereal I starts at Uncommon, II at Rare, III at Epic.
- {storage} **Storage**: A realm stores 6,000, 18,000, 40,000, then 80,000 wheat and labor by castle level.
- {research} **Research steps**: Each research rank adds 20% output or 40% storage, and each hut rank 20% population.
  `25% / 50% → 20% / 40%`
- {rations} **Rations**: Each Rations rank takes 0.2 wheat off a troop's deploy cost. `0.25 → 0.2`
- {depth-1} **The Ethereal depths**: Research the depths for less Essence.
  `160,000 / 400,000 / 900,000 → 100,000 / 250,000 / 500,000`
- {war-hall} **Training buildings**: A training building costs Essence and labor and comes with rank I.
  `3,000 labor → 6,000 Essence + 1,500 labor`

### Fixes

- {military} **Back to your army**: After a won clear or an order from a card, the panel returns to your army.
- {menu} **Panels and Back**: Every Menu row is one tap target, every panel shares one frame, and Back never stops on a
  panel you cannot see.
- {attack} **Site cards**: Select an army on the map, then tap a site: no army picker, the guard shown as a token, and
  standing guards without stamina bars.
