## All modes

### New

- {play} **A new app**: Play, Season, What's new and You across the top, along the bottom on a phone, and Market opens
  Realms' marketplace. Play leads with your next step, then each mode's games; Season shows each mode's board and your
  Blitz rating; You holds your card, your finished games and Settings. A finished game opens Results on your score card,
  and a phone plays either way up.
- {person} **Sign in your way**: Sign in with Discord or a code sent to your email, and choose the name every player
  sees. Settings lists your devices, up to eight, and removing one signs it out. Link a payout wallet (Ready, Braavos or
  Controller, or Ready by email if you have none) with an emailed code; it can be paid at once.
- {map} **One HUD for every mode**: Your holdings on the left, one kind per tab, with a star to pin a realm; the
  selected realm's card and the minimap under them; Next up, the events, the picked tile and chat on the right. Every
  game opens on the world map. On a phone, Blitz shows Empire, Map, Log, Chat and Details as tabs.
- {layers} **A new look**: Every panel and window, from Build to the leaderboard, is redrawn in one style and one
  readable typeface, with a painted icon for every resource, troop, relic and action. Structure colours are gone.
- {star} **Next up**: The HUD names one thing to do now, alerts first, with a button where the step has one, or what to
  do where it has none. In a Blitz it is the step that scores most: open a crate beside your army, explore, take a camp,
  or level up first when an attack would wipe your army out, and after a loss where to raise the next army. In Frontier
  it is what the day calls for: barracks, a deploy, a guard in reach, a tier, the castle, research, exploring.
- {settings} **Keys**: B, P, M and T open Build, Production, Military and Transfer, Space takes Next up's step, and I
  selects your next army at full stamina. Settings keeps the list of keys behind Show.
- {move} **The next order**: Give a moving army its next order; it leaves as soon as the first one lands.
- {map-realm} **Map tokens**: Every army, realm and site wears a token in its side's colour: its troops' count and tier
  (a beast guard shows its beast instead), an army's stamina bar, and a dashed ring when nothing guards a structure that
  can be attacked. Hover or select it and its plate opens in its place: an army's player, home and exact stamina, a
  realm's guards in the order an attacker meets them, a site's own name and what it holds.
- {realm} **Ownership rings**: Select a structure to ring everything its owner holds, yours or another player's.
- {explore} **What a right-click does**: With an army selected, hover a tile: it says what a right-click does there,
  what it costs and what it scores.
- {transfer} **One Transfer window**: Transfer, Arrivals, Automation and Balances share one window. Send goods to
  several holdings at once, each total split evenly, or right-click a holding of yours on the map to send there. Each
  shipment goes on its own and says when it lands; one the game refused stays chosen for Send to try again.
- {military} **Deploy by troop column**: Deploying shows a column per troop with its three tiers in place, +500 and Max,
  and what it costs. The deploy map shows only the six tiles an army can step out onto.
- {chronicles} **Events and the Log**: Events shows the game's three latest, the important ones first, with Mine and All
  a switch away. The Log tells each action once, in plain words, and a capture is told to both sides.
- {leaderboard} **The leaderboard**: One ranked list of every player, kept current, each column with the points it
  grants. The top bar's chip shows your rank and points.
- {build} **Build cards**: A card shows the building, how many you have and its actions. Hover it for what it costs
  against what you hold, what one more gives and what it burns.
- {attack} **The attack forecast**: It names both sides, what each loses and who survives, and a certain loss says what
  to do instead.
- {notifications} **Notifications**: Choose Off, Important or All. Important tells you when your Frontier day ends in an
  hour, your Blitz starts, a message reaches you, or you are attacked or a holding of yours is taken; All adds every
  clear, fight and build, and a rested army.
- {victory} **The game's end**: A fractured veil spreads from the spires, and the map, its doorway and Game finished
  lead to Results, where standings stay final. From Results, See the end map lets anyone scout the map as the game
  ended.
- {realm-3} **A redrawn world**: Realms grow by level, from Canvas & Thatch to the imperial citadel, with your Order on
  their banners. Villages, outposts, fallen realms, the T1 Knight and workshops have new models; mist marks the edge of
  explored land, and shadows hold still.
- {chat} **Block a player**: Block a player in direct messages, and nothing they send reaches you; they are never told.

### Fixes

- {move} **Selection holds**: An army stays selected after its order lands, a selected structure that moves takes the
  selection with it, and closing a card keeps your place.
- {stamina} **Numbers stay put**: Stores and stamina no longer blank out after an action, and an army can take its next
  order as soon as the last one applies.
- {check} **One press, one send**: A second press no longer sends an action twice, and an action the game has not
  answered yet says so.
- {map} **A map that fails says so**: If the map cannot be drawn on the way in, the game says so and offers Open realm
  beside Try again, so your realm stays in reach.
- {chat} **Chat reconnects**: Chat comes back by itself after a dropped connection.
- {move} **Armies on the ground**: A marching army keeps its feet on the ground instead of floating and bobbing above
  the map.
- {exploration-relic-1} **Relics end on time**: A timed relic starts when you apply it and ends at its last second.
- {watch} **Show frames both**: Show on a crate, a camp or a Hyperstructure frames your army and its target.

## Frontier

### New

- {realm} **Frontier opens**: A new land every day, and your realm keeps what it earns. Free to play: your realm is
  founded as you first enter, and no player can attack it.
- {calendar} **Days of 8 to 24 hours**: Each day lasts 8 to 24 hours, drawn for the whole season. When it ends, the map
  and every army on it are gone; your realm, its buildings and its research stay.
- {realm-0} **Your realm**: A new realm starts with 1,500 troops, 4,500 wheat and 2,000 labor. Farms make wheat,
  barracks train troops, workshops make labor and each hut adds 6 population, which every other building takes. Each
  copy of a building costs more than the last, and pulling one down returns half its price.
- {realm-3} **The castle**: Raise your castle for 4,000, 15,000, then 40,000 labor. Each level opens another ring of
  plots, 60 in all, fields one more army, from 3 to 6, and deploys more troops at once, from 3,000 to 60,000.
- {research} **Research**: At the castle, each building type climbs from common to legendary for Essence and labor, and
  a tier lifts every building of the type at once. Farms and workshops make 25% more or store more, barracks train 25%
  more or deploy for a quarter less wheat, and each hut tier houses a quarter more. A choice is final for the season.
- {war-hall} **Training buildings**: Once your barracks reach rare, raise one War hall, Supply yard, Scouts' lodge and
  Hearth. Each of their tiers starts every new army a tier higher in Battle, Logistics, Scouting or Homecoming.
- {build} **Build on a plot**: Tap a plot to build there. Each card says what it makes, what locks it and why its price
  rose, a refused build says why and puts the fix on its button, and every building's panel shows what it makes and
  holds.
- {deploy} **Deploy**: Barracks train for free, and each troop costs 2 wheat when it deploys, onto the tile you pick
  beside your realm; Deploy shows the price and how far the wheat left lets the army march. Two of your armies side by
  side can merge, keeping the lower stamina.
- {map} **Frontier's screen**: On a desktop, Frontier plays on the HUD Blitz plays: your realm's panel, a ring per store
  and your LORDS, over a row per army with its exact stamina; Next up, the events and chat on the right; the day's clock
  in the top bar; Research, Production, Today and Season at the foot. Orders go from the map, by right-click. A phone
  keeps a strip of stores at the top, a bar of places at the foot and one sheet at a time.
- {stamina} **Stamina**: An army holds 120 stamina and recovers 1 every 2 minutes. Revealing a tile costs 20, a step
  over known ground 10, and an attack needs 30.
- {lords} **Refill**: Refill an army's stamina for 1 LORDS a missing point.
- {wheat} **Wheat on the march**: Every step and every reveal costs 0.02 wheat per troop.
- {explore} **Explore for labor and Essence**: Each reveal pays Essence or labor by the army's size, more in the depths,
  and sends it home to your realm. Your realm's six neighbours start each day explored.
- {beast-troll} **Sites to clear**: Trolls, rifts, ruins and roaming beasts hold the land's riches, and each shows its
  guard and its payout before you attack: a Troll pays labor, a rift Essence, a ruin its chest. A site pays once: a
  cleared one leaves a standard that shows what the clear paid, and armies can walk onto it.
- {beast-troll} **Beasts**: Beasts roam the land in two strengths, as likely as each other: a Cyclops, 300 to 350 troops
  on the surface, or a stronger Wraith, 450 to 550, with more of each deeper down. They are the weakest guards and pay
  only XP. Trolls stand at every depth. A Dark fey hovers above each rift. Ruins are guarded by an Ammit on the surface,
  a Basilisk in Ethereal I, a Wyvern in Ethereal II and a Hydra in Ethereal III, with low drifting mist across their
  floors.
- {dice} **Fights roll dice**: Each side rolls a d20 and hits up to 20% harder; the ground never helps either side.
- {attack} **Every fight ends on a result**: Victory, Army lost or Guard holds, with both sides' troops before and after
  and what a clear paid. Frontier asks before you disband an army.
- {chest} **Chests in the wild**: Each newly revealed surface tile has a 1 in 10,000 chance of a chest of LORDS, found
  only while the season's pool can fund it: one common share at today's price, set aside as it is found. Open it with an
  army beside it before the day ends; the LORDS go to your realm.
- {lords} **Ruins pay LORDS**: Clearing a ruin pays its chest in LORDS, priced each day from the season's pool. The
  Season page shows the pool, today's price and its ceiling.
- {star} **Armies learn**: An army spends its XP on the attribute tiers you choose, up to tier 5: Battle hits up to
  twice as hard, Logistics carries up to 150 more stamina, each Scouting tier adds 10%, 20%, 30%, then 40% to the
  Trolls, rifts or beasts you pick for it, so all four on one kind find twice as many. On a desktop, Upgrade on an
  army's row opens its tiers once its XP buys one.
- {people} **Homecoming**: When a day ends, an army with Homecoming sends 3% to 30% of its survivors home, by its tier.
- {mountain} **Ethereal depths**: Research opens the Ethereal depths; an army enters one from your realm's spire.
- {flame} **Shrines and wells**: A shrine grants XP and a well refills an army's stamina.
- {lords} **Your LORDS**: Withdraw the LORDS your realm holds to your payout wallet, until the season's claim window
  closes.
- {labor} **Realm labor**: Realms in your payout wallet give labor once a day: add one by its number, then Claim all.
- {chronicles} **Today and the season**: Today totals your day; Season ranks every realm, and you can visit one.

## Blitz

### New

- {military} **One troop type per realm**: Each realm starts with 1,000 T1 troops in its garrison and 1,000 in reserve,
  all of the one type it was dealt, instead of 3,500 of each type. `10,500 → 2,000`
- {military} **Merge across realms**: Your armies from different realms merge when they hold the same troop type and
  tier.
- {chest} **Themed relic crates**: A crate holds one pack of three relics, War, Explore, Produce or Fortress, revealed
  when you open it. One crate in five holds its pack at level II.
- {labor-production-relic-1} **Labor relics**: Labor Production relics come in Produce packs: level I adds 20% labor
  production, level II 40%.
- {essence} **Dormant spires**: One reveal in two hundred raises a dormant spire, which pays its finder's realm 1,000
  Essence plus an active Exploration relic's bonus.
- {lords} **Paid entry**: Blitz entry is paid in LORDS from your linked payout wallet, and free signup is gone. A slot
  shows how many registered; your entry reads Registered, then Seated, or Not seated and refunded.
- {calendar} **Entry closes before the start**: Entry closes five minutes before the games start. A game opens on time
  once its whole roster is seated, in games of up to 24; until then, every action says when play opens.
- {build} **Build order**: On a desktop, a Build order panel under Next up names each realm's next building on a
  recommended order, with the button that builds it. Levelling the realm up comes first, then a worker hut when
  population is 3 from full.
- {chat} **The lobby**: Registered players write in their slot's lobby chat, and the roster shows each seat's rating.
- {lords} **Where an entry goes**: 90% of every entry funds the season's reward chests and 10% the treasury; what the
  chests leave at the season's end joins the season prize. Games no longer pay prizes one by one, and sponsorship is
  gone.
- {chest} **LORDS in chests**: Every player's chest can award one entry's worth of LORDS, or a sword or shield credit;
  LORDS only while the chest reserve can pay them.
- {attack} **Sword and shield**: At entry, a sword doubles your rating gain and a shield halves your rating loss, each
  for the price of an entry.
- {leaderboard} **Every rated game counts**: A rated game moves your MMR however many play. `6 players → any`
- {leaderboard} **Your MMR if it ended now**: In a paid, rated game the leaderboard shows each player's rating change if
  the game ended now.
- {person} **Rewards follow your seat**: Results, chests and season prizes go to the wallet your seat was played with,
  even after you change your payout wallet.

### Balance

- {wheat} **Wheat on the march**: Moving or exploring costs wheat per troop per hex, so a big army needs farms behind
  it. `0.03 → 0.05`
- {explore} **Explore with small armies**: A reveal pays a share of the army's strength (troops times tier: 1, 3 or 9),
  up to 3,000. At the cap it finds 150, 300 or 600 Essence, as before.
- {labor} **Labor from exploring**: At the cap a reveal finds 300 or 600 labor. `500 / 1,000 → 300 / 600`
- {donkey} **Donkeys from exploring**: At the cap a reveal finds 300 donkeys. `500 → 300`
- {camp} **Guards**: Camp and Essence Rift guards hold 999 to 1,499 troops of a random type; hyperstructure guards 1,499
  to 1,999. `800–1,600 → 999–1,499`
- {production} **No labor production**: Labor no longer pays for production: resources and troops come from their
  resource recipes only.

### Fixes

- {hyperstructure} **Captured hyperstructures pay at once**: A hyperstructure you take pays you from the moment you take
  it, and its shares follow it on every capture.

## Eternum

### Fixes

- {trade} **Trade opens**: Opening Trade no longer stops the game, and every resource and relic in the market shows its
  painting.
