## All modes

### New

- {play} **A new app**: Play, Season, What's new and You across the top, and Market opens Realms' marketplace.
- {person} **Sign in your way**: Sign in with Discord or a code sent to your email; link a wallet only to be paid.
- {map} **One HUD for every mode**: Frontier, Blitz and Eternum share one HUD, drawn with painted icons, with the orders
  as round buttons between its columns.
- {move} **The next order**: Give a moving army its next order; it leaves as soon as the first one lands.
- {star} **Next up**: The HUD names the one step that scores most now, and the leaderboard shows each activity's points.
- {map-realm} **Ownership rings and plates**: Select a structure to ring everything its owner holds; plates name what
  stands on the map.

## Frontier

### New

- {realm} **Frontier opens**: A new land every day, and your realm keeps what it earns. Free to play, and no player can
  attack your realm.
- {calendar} **Days of 8 to 24 hours**: Each day lasts 8 to 24 hours, drawn for the whole season. When it ends, the map
  and every army on it are gone.
- {military} **Armies per realm**: A realm fields 3 armies at once; its castle raises that to 4, 5 and 6.
- {stamina} **Stamina**: An army holds 150 stamina and recovers 1 every 2 minutes. Revealing a tile costs 30, a step
  over known ground 10.
- {wheat} **Wheat on the march**: Every step and every reveal costs 0.02 wheat per troop. A new realm starts with 4,500
  wheat.
- {camp} **Sites to clear**: Camps, rifts and ruins hold the land's riches, and each shows its exact guard before you
  attack.
- {dice} **Fights roll dice**: Each side rolls a d20 and hits up to 20% harder; the ground never helps either side.
- {lords} **Ruins pay LORDS**: Clearing a ruin pays its chest in LORDS, priced each day from the season's pool.
- {star} **Armies learn**: An army spends its XP on the attribute tiers you choose, up to tier 5.
- {people} **Homecoming**: When a day ends, an army with Homecoming sends 3% to 30% of its survivors home, by its tier.
- {build} **Build to stay**: Research and the buildings on your realm's plots stay with it from one day to the next.

## Blitz

### New

- {military} **One troop type per realm**: Each realm starts with 1,000 T1 troops in its garrison and 1,000 in reserve,
  all of the one type it was dealt, instead of 3,500 of each type. `10,500 → 2,000`
- {military} **Merge across realms**: Your armies from different realms merge when they hold the same troop type and
  tier.
- {chest} **Themed relic crates**: A crate holds one pack of three relics, named before you open it: War, Explore,
  Produce or Fortress. One crate in five holds its pack at level II.
- {labor-production-relic-1} **Labor relics**: Labor Production relics come in Produce packs: level I adds 20% labor
  production, level II 40%.
- {essence} **Dormant spires**: One reveal in two hundred raises a dormant spire, which pays its finder's realm 1,000
  Essence plus an active Exploration relic's bonus.
- {calendar} **Entry closes before the start**: Entry closes five minutes before the games start. A game opens on time
  once its whole roster is seated, in games of up to 24.
- {lords} **Where an entry goes**: 80% of every entry funds the season's prize pool, 10% the reward chests and 10% the
  treasury. Games no longer pay prizes one by one, and sponsorship is gone. `70% → 80%`
- {chest} **LORDS in chests**: A chest from the top three rank bands may hold 4, 2 or 1 entries' worth of LORDS.
- {attack} **Sword and shield**: At entry, a sword doubles your rating gain and a shield halves your rating loss, each
  for the price of an entry.
- {leaderboard} **Every rated game counts**: A rated game moves your MMR however many play. `6 players → any`

### Balance

- {wheat} **Wheat on the march**: Moving or exploring costs wheat per troop per hex, so a big army needs farms behind
  it. `0.03 → 0.05`
- {explore} **Explore with small armies**: A reveal pays a share of the army's strength (troops times tier: 1, 3 or 9),
  up to 3,000. At the cap it finds 150, 300 or 600 Essence, as before.
- {labor} **Labor from exploring**: At the cap a reveal finds 300 or 600 labor. `500 / 1,000 → 300 / 600`
- {donkey} **Donkeys from exploring**: At the cap a reveal finds 300 donkeys. `500 → 300`
- {camp} **Guards**: Camp and Essence Rift guards hold 999 to 1,499 troops of a random type; hyperstructure guards 1,499
  to 1,999. `800–1,600 → 999–1,499`
- {production} **No labor production**: Labor no longer pays for production: resources and troops come from their
  resource recipes only.

### Fixes

- {move} **Selection holds**: Your army stays selected after its order lands.
- {stamina} **Relics end on time**: A relic's bonus ends at its last second, not at the next tick.
