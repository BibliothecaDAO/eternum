title: "Phases and brackets"
summary: "Days split into phases, battles gain a timer, and Blitz drops storage."
---

## All modes

### New

- {sun} **Phases**: An Eternum day is six phases: a minute each in Blitz, 12 minutes in Eternum.
- {attack} **Battle timer**: Damage now sets a battle timer, with refunds; moving troops affects it.

## Blitz

### New

- {lords} **Gladiator bracket**: A Blitz bracket with a 250 LORDS entry.

### Balance

- {build} **No storage**: Storage and storehouses are gone from Blitz.
- {hyperstructure} **Hyperstructures**: The victory points a second for holding a hyperstructure changed.
