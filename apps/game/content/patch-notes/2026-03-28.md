## All modes

### New

- {leaderboard} **MMR leaderboard**: A podium, badges and Find me; the board fills during the game, not only at the end.
- {chronicles} **Post-game review**: A game's review screens, its awards and a recap card to share.
- {chat} **Realtime chat**: Chat in mainnet Blitz games, with grouped messages, direct-message toasts and emoji.

## Blitz

### Balance

- {calendar} **Game length**: A Blitz lasts 60 minutes (10 Eternum days). `90 → 60`
- {labor} **Starting labor**: Each realm starts with 1,500 labor. `1,200 → 1,500`
- {wood} **Starting materials**: Wood 360, coal 240, copper 120, donkeys 500, and 5,000 T1 troops.
- {camp} **Camp stockpiles**: Camps start with 500 wheat, 5,000 labor and 1,000 donkeys.
- {realm} **Start as a City**: Every realm starts at City level; the Settlement upgrade is gone.
- {production} **Production**: Resources, Essence and labor are produced at 2 a second. `1 → 2`
- {donkey} **Donkeys**: Donkey production is 5 a second. `3 → 5`
- {essence} **T2 and T3 buildings**: Their Essence cost is halved: 300 for T2 and the T3 resource buildings, 600 for T3
  military.
- {explore} **Exploration rewards**: Reveals find 150, 300 or 600 Essence, 500 or 1,000 labor, or 500 donkeys; T1 troops
  are no longer found.
- {essence} **Essence Rifts**: One reveal in 40 finds a rift, and a rift produces 20 Essence a second. `10 → 20`
- {stamina} **Stamina**: Armies deploy with 30 stamina and gain 30 a phase, 180 an Eternum day. `20 → 30`
- {victory} **Victory points**: Explore a tile 5, claim a world structure 250, open a relic crate 250, claim a
  hyperstructure 1,000; holding a hyperstructure stays 1 to 6 a second.
