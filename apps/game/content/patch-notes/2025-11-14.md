## Blitz

### Balance

- {wheat} **Wheat on the march**: Moving or exploring costs 0.03 wheat per troop.
- {essence} **Essence Rifts**: One reveal in 50 finds a rift, and a rift produces 10 Essence a second. `5 → 10`
- {people} **Population**: The Keep and Worker's Huts each add 6 population. `5 → 6`
- {wheat} **Farms**: A farm makes 6 wheat a second. `5 → 6`
- {military} **Troop wheat**: Producing T2 troops costs 3 wheat a second (was 4), T3 troops 4 (was 7).
- {labor-production-relic-1} **Labor Production relics**: Removed from Blitz.
- {flame} **Scorched ground**: Crossbowmen fight better there and Paladins worse; Knights are unaffected.
- {crossbowman-t1} **Scorched realms**: Realms on scorched ground start with Crossbowmen.
- {stamina} **Snow**: Knights and Crossbowmen no longer pay extra stamina on snow.
- {victory} **Exploring**: Exploring a tile is worth 25 victory points. `50 → 25`
