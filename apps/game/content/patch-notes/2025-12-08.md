## Blitz

### Balance

- {labor} **Starting labor**: Each realm starts with 1,200 labor.
- {camp} **Camp stockpiles**: Camps start with 500 wheat, 2,500 labor and 200 donkeys.
- {victory} **Exploring**: Exploring a tile is worth 10 victory points. `25 → 10`
- {production} **Recipes**: Resource recipes, troop upgrade inputs, donkey output and T2 and T3 building costs changed.
