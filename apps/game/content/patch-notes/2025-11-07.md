## Blitz

### Balance

- {military} **Army size**: An army holds at most 30,000 troops. `100,000 → 30,000`
- {realm} **Realm upgrades**: Upgrades need less labor, resources and Essence.
- {chest} **Relic crates**: Opening a relic crate is worth 1,000 victory points.
- {explore} **Exploration bundles**: Troops found while exploring come in bundles of 1,000. `2,500 → 1,000`
- {essence} **Rifts and camps**: One reveal in 30 finds an Essence Rift or a camp. `1/20 → 1/30`
- {military} **Tier strength**: T2 troops are 3 times as strong as T1, and T3 9 times.
- {production} **Resource and labor modes**: The two ways to build and produce are named resource and labor.
