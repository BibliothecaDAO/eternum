## Blitz

### Balance

- {crossbowman-t1} **Crossbow range**: Crossbowmen attack from 2 hexes away; Knights and Paladins stay adjacent.
- {crossbowman-t1} **Ranged pokes**: A range-2 attack deals 70% damage to field armies and 30% to structure guards, with
  no biome modifier, no counter-damage and no refunds. Structures still need adjacency to claim.
- {stamina} **Ranged stamina**: A range-2 attack costs the attacker 50 stamina and the defender 20; range 1 stays 50
  and 40.
- {knight-t1} **Knights take walls**: Knights deal 15% more assaulting structure guards from an adjacent hex, and Knight
  guards take 15% less damage.
- {military} **Tier strength**: T2 troops are 3 times as strong as T1, and T3 9 times.
- {stamina} **Stamina recovery**: Stamina recovers 30 a phase, 180 an Eternum day.
- {exploration-relic-1} **Relic timers**: Every timed relic lasts 3 Eternum days (18 minutes).
- {troop-production-relic-1} **Troop production relics**: Level I adds 15% troop production, level II 30%.
