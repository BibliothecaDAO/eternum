## Tripo intake supplement

Use `asset-brief.md` as the current visual authority and `revision-card.md` for follow-up edits. This compact section
can be added to an existing project asset record without duplicating the whole brief.

- Approved concept packet: front/back/profile/top paths, hashes, prompts and approval.
- Submitted horizontal/component subset and mapping to the complete concept.
- Provider route, rights/terms evidence, candidate allocation and verified credit allocation.
- CLI/model versions, exact parameters, seed values, dry-run payload and source-quality rationale.
- Task IDs, terminal outputs, raw model/map files, hashes and actual charges.
- Actual raw-mesh review evidence, user's approval and accepted repair scope.
- Blender part lineage, preserved maps/UVs, local repairs and rig/adapter status.
- Material/clay all-angle findings, including true top/bottom and intentional openings.
- Tested ROM limits, export round-trip, exact runtime files and scene integration evidence.
- Comparison URL/version, unresolved defects and production eligibility (separate statuses).
