# Knight pathway

Use a Knight-owned asset brief and namespace. Read the latest concept and feedback; the archived early Knight is not an
approved default or rig. Keep the common scale/quality contract without inheriting Paladin rider proportions or
contacts.

Skin includes anatomy, clothing, armor, cap and boots. Primary is a Knight weapon; secondary is a Knight forearm shield.
Preserve simple gloved grips if approved. Record right palm/grip, left forearm support and foot-sole frames; inspect
neutral wrists, shield interior, elbow direction and shoulder continuity. Shields have no rear arm loops/bands; use
direct forearm placement and the [equipment-fit checks](../equipment-fit.md). Item roots must remain independently
removable. Armor is part of the skin, so no hidden-body reconstruction program for modular armor is required.

Export/bind/neutral first, then affected arm/torso/leg ROM. Review sword edge/orientation, hand/bracer intersections,
shield knee/torso clearance and complete feet on ground. Fit every 1–6 formation at fixed human scale using swept shield
and weapon envelopes. Lower LOD must preserve hand/shield/contact interpretation, not just pass a triangle count.

Own a Knight controller/profile behind the existing unit runtime: idle, walk, run, attack and defend are separate
readiness cells. Reuse humanoid solvers and shared style; Knight guard/stride/melee tuning does not edit Crossbowman or
Paladin profiles. Melee/guard references and weapon motion classes determine reach and contact within authoritative
action timing. A new shield cosmetic tests the affected carry/guard/attack states and nearest transitions; it does not
commission other family animations. Follow [motion review](../procedural-animation.md) for an animation task.
