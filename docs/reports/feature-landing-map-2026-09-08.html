The way forward read off the moves, smallest first

Each step moves features onto a surface that already exists, so nothing has to be finished before the next step starts. Material, colour and type come after step six as their own pass.

  1. Right panel takes the army actions. Create a field army from spawn hexes with a picker at the hex; guard picker on the slot; readiness as one bar; attack preview in the panel's target section.Moves 5 rows. Deletes the Military window's reason to open for the common case.
  2. Drawer replaces the Build and Production windows. Picker at the plot; production controls inline on the building panel; the popover primitive learns to wait for a map click.Moves 4 rows. The 1320 px Build view survives only as the catalogue.
  3. Feed replaces toasts and the logistics window. Activity, battles, crate results, arrivals with claim, my orders, one chat line. Space jumps to the last row.Moves 6 rows, cuts 93 toast sites.
  4. Header carries time and standing. One clock, six numbers, attention count with next, rank with the gap. Day names and the percent pill go.Moves 5 rows, cuts 1.
  5. Scene and labels carry whose and how strong. Action highlights scoped to the selection; labels on the ladder; army models by size bucket in the Model Lab.Moves 2 rows, adds 1.
  6. Rail becomes the outliner. Counts per kind with attention, suggestions as a row, automation as a switch. Stress-tested at 10, 30 and 100 holdings.Moves 3 rows.
  7. Spectator follows a lord. Picker in the identity chip, rail as their holdings, labels relative to them.Adds 1 row, the largest single gap for casters.
  8. Transfers and the market move to drawers. Destination picked on the map, amounts in the drawer; Eternum's ledger drawer.Moves 4 rows.
  9. Material pass. Brand tokens, type, panel material, one token at a time on surfaces that now hold the right features.
How each step is judged: the feature is on its surface, the map stays visible and clickable, the click count is at or below the database's today column, and nothing that used to be readable stopped being readable.
