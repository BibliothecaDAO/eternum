Feature Landing Map

The frame is the HUD that ships today. Every feature keeps its home unless that home fails one of two tests: it hides the map for a task the map has to finish, or it breaks at a hundred realms and twenty-two resources. A feature that fails moves to the nearest existing surface. Two surfaces are added because nothing existing can take their job.

The way forward only the rows that fail a test move, smallest first

  1. Army creation finishes on the map. Spawn hexes when a structure is selected, a picker at the hex, the guard picker at the slot. The Military window stays as the catalogue.Fails the map test today. Moves 2 rows.
  2. Build finishes at the plot. Picker anchored to the plot; the popover primitive learns to wait for a map click. The Build window stays as the catalogue.Fails the map test today. Moves 1 row, fixes the class for every later drawer.
  3. Production and labor go inline on the building and castle panels.Fails the map test today. Moves 2 rows.
  4. The feed replaces toasts, arrivals and the activity list. Rows group per tick.Fails at a hundred realms today. Moves 3 rows, cuts 93 call sites.
  5. Scene highlights and labels scale. Selection and targets only; label budget; owner name once.Fails at 30 armies and 96 players today. Moves 2 rows.
  6. The structure list scales. Search, counts per category, sort by attention; armies as a section.Fails at a hundred realms today. Adds 1 row, moves 1.
  7. One clock and one attention count in the header.Three pills become one; attention badges become one count. Moves 2 rows, cuts 1.
  8. Transfers, troop moves and relics go to drawers from the panel.Fail the map test today. Moves 4 rows.
  9. Spectators follow a lord.Adds 1 row.
  10. Models by size. Model Lab work, independent of the HUD.Adds 1 row.
What does not move: leaderboard, settings, hints, identity, suggestions, the resource panel, the minimap, the quick actions row, the entry modal. Everything that already reads at scale and leaves the map alone stays exactly where it is.
