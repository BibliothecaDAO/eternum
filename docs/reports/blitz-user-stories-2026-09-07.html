The six lords of the hour

The RegularPlays most evenings. Wants a seat, the horn, and a first order inside the first minute. Judges the game by how little it makes them hunt.
The First-TimerArrived from a link with one hour to learn by doing. Reads the screen, not a manual. Every dead end costs a player.
The CommanderNine armies, ninety-five opponents. Lives on hotkeys, Tab and the minimap. Wants stamina, odds and range without opening anything.
The QuartermasterPlays the economy: labor, production, transfers timed to the delivery tick. Wants rates, not totals, and a warning before a shortage.
The ContenderChasing the top ten. Counts points per action, hunts hyperstructures and relics, and registers points before the window closes.
The WitnessSpectates live or reviews after. Follows one lord at a time and reads the same field with the same labels, minus the buttons.

Where a need lands

CHIPIdentity, game, phase and the clock. Top-left. The only place the match's time lives.
LEDGERResources with rates, this realm or the empire. Top-centre.
ALERTSAttacks, arrivals, rank and settings. Top-right.
RAILRealms and army groups as portrait tiles. Left edge, keys 1 to 9.
CARDThe selection, its commands as a Q W E grid, and the context on the right third. Bottom-centre.
MAPLabels, ghosts, rings and the cursor. The scene itself.
MINIMAPBottom-left, with the Local and World toggle and the lens keys.
FEEDEvents newest first as text on the scene, right edge. Replaces toasts and windows.

Spectator rule: one layout, one mount tree. Spectating removes buttons and adds a player list. It never branches the components.

Never

What the deck refuses to do, so that the stories above stay true.

A modal over the scene. Every window becomes a card tab, a drawer, or a feed row.
A spinner while the chain works. The ghost is the feedback. Confirmation is a colour change.
A confirmation dialog for a reversible action. The irreversible ones hold to confirm.
A toast. Toasts are a queue you cannot revisit. The feed is the queue.
A second clock. Percent, day and countdown collapse into one.
A page reload to change account or game. The chip handles identity in place.
