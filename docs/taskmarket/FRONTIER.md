# Frontier in one page

The primer every Frontier bounty in this folder shares. The rules, with their numbers, are at
[docs.realms.party/frontier](https://docs.realms.party/frontier); when this page and the docs disagree, the docs win.

## What it is

Frontier is free to play. Every day a new map opens around your realm, hidden in fog. Your armies walk into it, clear
what guards the land, and send home what they find. No other player can attack you. Play it at
[play.realms.party](https://play.realms.party).

## The day

1. **Deploy.** Barracks train troops at your realm. Deploying puts them beside it as an army.
2. **Explore.** The map is hidden until you walk it. What sits on a tile is decided the moment an army reveals it, so
   nobody can scout ahead. Every reveal sends Essence or labor home.
3. **Clear.** Trolls, rifts, ruins and beasts guard their sites. Each shows its guard and what it pays before you
   attack. No dice are rolled: a fight's forecast is its result.
4. **Bring it home.** Everything a reveal or a clear pays goes straight to your realm. An army never carries anything
   worth losing.

When the day ends, the map, every site and every army on it are gone. Your realm is raised again on the next map with
its buildings, research, troops and stores.

## What a reveal can find

Beasts, camps, rifts and ruins (each with a guard), shrines (a skill point) and wells (stamina), and now and then a
chest. A ruin is a fallen realm, the strongest guard, and its chest pays LORDS. Research opens the Ethereal depths below
the surface, reached through a spire beside your realm: deeper ground pays more and is guarded harder.

## What Frontier is not

No player-versus-player fights, no trade, no sending anything to another player. No relics, tribes, faith or
Hyperstructures: those belong to Blitz and Eternum.
